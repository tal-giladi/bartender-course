Guest scenarios

Seven situations every bartender meets. Each is a conversation with four decisions; each choice scores 2 (safe and professional), 1 (safe but weak) or 0 (poor), and some 0-choices are safety-critical. A situation passes with at least 6 of 8 points and no safety-critical choice. All guests are fictional; legal references were checked on 2026-10-18 and are not legal advice.
