Dilution lab

Ice chills mainly by melting, so chilling always adds water. This model draws dilution and temperature over time for each method, fitted to Dave Arnold's measurements of real cocktails (shaken sour about 63%, stirred Manhattan about 50%; about 20–25% at 0% ABV). My home measurement

Weigh the mix before ice and the strained drink after (lesson 03.5). For water-based mixes 1 g ≈ 1 ml. The comparison uses the method, ice and starting ABV set above. Some liquid always stays on the ice, so your number usually reads a few points low.
