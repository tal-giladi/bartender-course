Recipe scaler

Convert units, turn parts into ml, scale a recipe for N drinks and plan a batch with its water. Course values: 1 oz = 30 ml (bar convention; exact US oz 29.57 ml), 1 cl = 10 ml, barspoon 5 ml, dash 0.8 ml.

Convert a unit

ml = oz × 30 · oz = ml ÷ 30 (nearest ¼ oz) · cl × 10 · barspoon × 5 · dash × 0.8

Parts to ml

Write parts as 2, 0.75, ¾, 3/4 or 1 1/2.

ml of one ingredient = total ml × its parts ÷ sum of all parts
