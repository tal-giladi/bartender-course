# Simulations

Interactive practice tools that run in your browser. Each one is an educational model, not a real bar: it trains decisions, numbers and sequencing, not your hands. Open one from the lesson that links it, with the preset that lesson names.

| Simulation | What it trains | Used in | Presets |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Recipe scaler](recipe-scaler/index.html) | Unit conversions (ml, oz, cl, dash, barspoon), parts to ml, scaling for N drinks, batches with planned dilution, ABV after dilution, fridge or freezer, printable batch card | 03.1, 03.2, 08.3 | `convert`, `scale` (default), `batch` |
| [Dilution lab](dilution-lab/index.html) | Dilution and temperature by method (shake, stir, build, throw), time, ice size and condition and starting ABV; predict, compare with the model (Arnold-fitted), log home measurements | 03.5, 03.6, 03.7, 04.3, 04.4, 04.6, 04.8, 05.4 | `explore` (default), `shake`, `stir`, `build`, `throw`, `temperature` |
| [Recipe drill](recipe-drill/index.html) | Randomised recall of the 30 course specs (ingredients and ml within ±5 ml, method, glass and ice, garnish) with Leitner boxes; family sorting; 20-card final exam with score breakdown and copy-ready results | 05.1–05.8, 06.1–06.6, 06.8, 11.2 | `all` (default), `m05`, `m06`, `families`, `exam` |
