# Simulations

Interactive practice tools that run in your browser. Each one is an educational model, not a real bar: it trains decisions, numbers and sequencing, not your hands. Open one from the lesson that links it, with the preset that lesson names.

| Simulation | What it trains | Used in | Presets |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Recipe scaler](recipe-scaler/index.html) | Unit conversions (ml, oz, cl, dash, barspoon), parts to ml, scaling for N drinks, batches with planned dilution, ABV after dilution, fridge or freezer, printable batch card | 03.1, 03.2, 08.3 | `convert`, `scale` (default), `batch` |
| [Dilution lab](dilution-lab/index.html) | Dilution and temperature by method (shake, stir, build, throw), time, ice size and condition and starting ABV; predict, compare with the model (Arnold-fitted), log home measurements | 03.5, 03.6, 03.7, 04.3, 04.4, 04.6, 04.8, 05.4 | `explore` (default), `shake`, `stir`, `build`, `throw`, `temperature` |
| [Recipe drill](recipe-drill/index.html) | Randomised recall of the 30 course specs (ingredients and ml within ±5 ml, method, glass and ice, garnish) with Leitner boxes; family sorting; 20-card final exam with score breakdown and copy-ready results | 05.1–05.8, 06.1–06.6, 06.8, 11.2 | `all` (default), `m05`, `m06`, `families`, `exam` |
| [Costing calculator](costing-calculator/index.html) | Cost per serving with VAT removed and waste allowances, unit conversion, bottle and produce yield, formula price with VAT on/off and rounding, pour cost, gross profit, margin vs markup, 14-drink menu with sales-weighted pour cost and menu engineering, inventory variance in ml/%/ILS with flags, CSV export, marked final assignment | 10.1, 10.2, 10.3, 10.4, 10.5, 11.6 | `single` (default), `yield`, `variance`, `menu`, `exam` |
| [Guest scenarios](guest-scenarios/index.html) | Branching guest conversations for seven situations, 2/1/0 scoring with safety-critical choices scored separately, your path vs the model path with the rule behind it, copy-results block | 09.2, 09.3, 09.4, 09.5, 09.7, 11.5 | `underage`, `intoxicated`, `unclear`, `allergy`, `complaint`, `strong`, `alcohol-free`, `exam` (no preset = situation picker) |
| [Service shift](service-sim/index.html) | Multi-ticket bar service: queue decisions, passes, 4 tins and 2 mixing glasses, dead-drink rule, stock-outs (barback, fetch, squeeze, 86 with a substitute), sent-back remake, end-of-shift count and order, debrief with accuracy, ticket time, waste, consumption and error log, exam score | 08.4, 08.5, 08.8, 11.4 | `tutorial`, `practice` (default), `rush`, `stockout`, `exam` |
