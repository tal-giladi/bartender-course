Service shift

Tickets arrive; you make the drinks with the station's actions and serve each ticket when all its drinks are ready. The shift clock moves only when you act: every action adds its fixed time from lesson 08.5 (glass 2 s, ice 3 s, pour 4 s first / 2 s same bottle in a row, top 3 s, muddle 8 s, shake 12 s and stir 20 s for one or two at once, stir in glass 5 s, strain 3 s, garnish 4 s, beer 8 s, wine 6 s, rinse 3 s, bin 2 s, serve 3 s). Take your time to decide: only the sequence counts. Station: 4 tins, 2 mixing glasses.
