# Instructor notes — 08.3 Batching and pre-dilution

- **Objective:** pre-mix (no water, mixed to order) vs pre-diluted (water in, poured straight); what stays out; planned dilution by serving temperature; ABV after dilution vs freezing point; full batch label.
- **Likely confusion:** why freezer batches use 20–25% water when stirring adds about 50%. The colder the serve, the less harsh it tastes and the less water it needs; and the batch can only be as cold as where it is stored.
- **Misconception:** "alcohol never freezes in a freezer". A diluted Manhattan at about 24% ABV freezes near −11 °C; a 40%-gin Martini batch with 20% water (30.7%) is borderline at −18 °C.
- **Expected hands-on result:** worksheet as in the solution (Manhattan × 24: 680 ml water, 99.1 ml serving, 23.8% ABV); blind test of the tea sour pre-mix usually inconclusive; pre-diluted batch about 4–5 °C vs about 0–1 °C stirred.
- **Extension:** ask for a freezer Negroni plan: work out ABV after 20% water with typical bottle ABVs and decide whether a −18 °C freezer is safe.
