# Instructor notes — 08.1 Opening and closing the bar

- **Objective:** move from the one-station drill of 01.7 to a whole-bar opening and close: temperature log, machines, stock pull to par, briefing, cash-up (named only), restock and handover note.
- **Likely confusion:** 4 °C vs 5 °C. FOODSAFE (BC) uses 0–4 °C for coolers, the CDC 40 °F (≈ 4.4 °C) for cut fruit, U.S. food codes 41 °F (5 °C). The lesson teaches "0–4 °C, act above 4–5 °C" and leaves Israeli rules to Module 9.
- **Misconception:** "closing is cleaning". Closing also restocks, counts, logs and hands over; a learner who only cleans has done half of it.
- **Expected hands-on result:** first home opening 12–20 min, second ≤ 15 min; close ≤ 12 min. Most missed steps are the temperature log, labels and the handover note.
- **Extension:** have the learner swap checklists with someone else and run each other's; unclear steps show up immediately.
- **Link forward:** 08.2 turns the prep step into a prep list; 08.4 explains par and the order list; 08.6 covers the machines; 08.7 the briefing and 86 list.
