# Instructor notes — 08.6 Commercial bar equipment and safety

- **Objective:** what each machine does and its daily check (glasswasher, ice machine, soda gun, POS, draught), CO2 hazards and alarm response, keg handling, everyday hazards.
- **Volatility:** implementation. Temperatures (Food Code 4-501.112 via Minnesota rules), cleaning intervals (Parts Town/Manitowoc six months; Brewers Association two weeks for lines) and Coca-Cola's bar gun guide may change; recheck yearly.
- **Likely confusion:** high- vs low-temperature glasswashers. The 82 °C figure applies only to hot-water sanitising machines; chemical machines are checked by sanitiser level.
- **Misconception:** "a brave colleague goes in to help". The lesson is explicit: never enter or re-enter a gassed cellar; second-rescuer casualties are the reason.
- **Expected hands-on result:** 8–10 of 12 hazards on the first read; usually missed: glass scoop on the ice machine, wine in the ice bin, the untrained gas change.
- **Extension:** ask the learner to write a one-page "first shift safety questions" list to ask a new employer (where is the gas alarm, who changes cylinders, where are the chemicals, where is the first-aid kit).
- **Link forward:** Module 9 covers Israeli food-business and workplace-safety requirements; Module 7 covers pouring draught.
