# Instructor notes — 08.2 Mise en place: prep, storage and shelf life

- **Objective:** a prep list from a forecast (drinks × ml + 10% buffer − in-date stock → fruit and grams), prep order, storage and labelling, and a shelf-life table where every limit is tagged official, producer or judgment.
- **Likely confusion:** where the buffer goes. It is added once to the total need, before subtracting fridge stock; stock is never "buffered".
- **Misconception:** "shelf life is a fact". Only the time/temperature limits (CDC 2 h, Food Code 7 days ≤ 5 °C for ready-to-eat TCS food) are official; syrup and juice keep-times are judgment. Push learners to label each claim.
- **Expected hands-on result:** prep list 1.3 L lime (44 limes), 300 ml lemon (9 lemons), 500 ml syrup to make (about 308 g sugar + 308 g water); mini prep 25–35 min; uncovered twists visibly dry within 2 h.
- **Extension:** give a quiet-night forecast (half the numbers) and ask what changes besides the amounts (juice pressed once, smaller garnish trays, syrup not made at all).
- **Link forward:** 08.3 batches; 08.4 uses the same items for counts and waste logs; Module 10 turns yield into cost.
