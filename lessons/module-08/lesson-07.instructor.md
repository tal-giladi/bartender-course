# Instructor notes — 08.7 Communication and teamwork on shift

- **Objective:** call-outs (behind, corner, hot, knife, glass, heard, 86) in English and Hebrew; check-back for spoken orders and requests; specific early barback requests; the 86 chain (manager, servers, board, POS); station sharing and handovers.
- **Likely confusion:** "glass!" vs "behind!". "Glass!" announces breakage and stops ice use; it is not a warning that you carry glassware.
- **Misconception:** "repeating orders looks unprofessional". It is standard practice in safety-critical teams (TeamSTEPPS closed-loop communication) and it prevents remakes.
- **Expected hands-on result:** with music, one or two modifiers missed before check-back in eight orders; 8/8 after check-back is the target. Calls within 2 seconds by the second round.
- **Extension:** run the check-back drill with two partners calling orders at the same time; the learner must queue them (08.5) and check back each one.
- **Note:** Hebrew call-outs are professional judgment; venues vary. Order 2 on the drill sheet links forward to Module 9 (health and allergy requests).
- **Link forward:** 08.8 uses barback requests and 86s inside the simulation; Module 9 covers guests, intoxication and refusing service.
