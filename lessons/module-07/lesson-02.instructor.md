# Instructor notes — 07.2 Pouring beer: draught, bottle and can

- **Objective:** learner pours with the tilt-then-upright method to a 2–3 cm head and separates technique faults from system faults.
- **Likely confusion:** "controlling" the tap by half-opening it; the difference between the liquid line and the rim.
- **Misconception:** a full glass with no head is better value. The BA notes that foam is about 25% beer and that brim-full is over-pouring.
- **Expected hands-on result:** first pours overshoot by 20–40 g; last five within ±10 g; 500 ml pours about 8–12 s from a bottle. Soda shows only a small foam difference; beer a large one.
- **Extension:** watch a bartender pour three draught beers (in person or on a reputable video) and note angle, upright point, head size and whether the nozzle touched the glass.
- **Safety:** wet floors; chipped glasses replaced, never served. Keg and CO2 handling are deliberately left to Module 8.
