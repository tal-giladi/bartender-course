# Instructor notes — 07.4 Opening and serving wine and sparkling wine

- **Objective:** learner opens still and sparkling wine safely and quietly, serves at the right temperature and stores opened bottles correctly. This is the lesson 06.3 refers to for opening sparkling wine.
- **Likely confusion:** turning the cork vs turning the bottle; "room temperature" in a hot Israeli bar; ice-only vs ice-and-water buckets.
- **Misconception:** the pop is part of the celebration. Both CMS and UMC treat a quiet release as the standard; a pop is wasteful and a safety risk.
- **Expected hands-on result:** still wine under about 60 s and quiet by the third run; ice bucket about 6–10 °C after 20 min vs fridge about 15–18 °C.
- **Extension:** open one real sparkling bottle under supervision (18+), following the checklist, and pour six glasses in two stages without foaming over.
- **Safety:** the WARNING on corks is non-negotiable; a learner who scores 0 on pointing or thumb control repeats the drill. Sabrage is explicitly excluded.
