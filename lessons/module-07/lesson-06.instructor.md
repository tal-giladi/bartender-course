# Instructor notes — 07.6 Recommend a drink: customer role-play

- **Objective:** module practical piece. Learner recommends beer, wine, sparkling and zero-proof drinks with a four-step routine and handles the safety-critical requests.
- **Likely confusion:** "strong" (flavour vs alcohol); "dry" (not sweet vs not sour); when to state ABV.
- **Misconception:** a polite request from an intoxicated guest can be met with a "weaker" drink. Any alcohol is a fail on guest 9.
- **Expected hands-on result:** 14–17/20 on a first attempt; misses typically one option only, missing ABV on NA products, IPA for "not bitter". Guests 4, 9, 10 must be correct (pass/fail).
- **Extension:** add five more guests from the learner's own experience (for example, a guest who wants "something like Aperol but not Aperol", or a sulphite-sensitive guest) and write model answers.
- **Notes:** the drinks list is fictional and uses generic styles with example ABVs; no real products or prices. The routine, translation table and food rules are professional judgment built on WSET's food-component list and CMS service standards.
