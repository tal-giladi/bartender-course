# Instructor notes — 07.5 Alcohol-free beer, wine and drinks

- **Objective:** learner reads alcohol-free labels exactly, explains how NA beer and wine are made and builds a short zero-proof offer.
- **Likely confusion:** UK "alcohol-free" (≤0.05%) vs common international "alcohol-free" (<0.5%) vs US "non-alcoholic" (<0.5%); Israeli labels show a number, which is what matters.
- **Misconception:** "0.5% is the same as zero". For a pregnant guest or someone avoiding alcohol completely it is not; state the number and let the guest decide.
- **Expected hands-on result:** worty/sweet notes in restricted-fermentation beers, thinner/less aromatic in dealcoholised ones; sugar per 100 ml varies widely; zero-proof serves improved by bitterness or bite.
- **Extension:** price the four zero-proof serves later in Module 10 and compare their margin with the cocktails.
- **Notes:** the UK consulted on raising the alcohol-free threshold to 0.5%; the 2018 guidance was still the published version on 2026-10-17. "בירה שחורה" (malt soft drink) is labelled professional judgment. Minors and NA beer are deliberately left to Module 9.
