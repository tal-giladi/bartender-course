# Instructor notes — 07.3 Wine basics: major grapes, styles and Israeli wine

- **Objective:** learner describes the eight principal grapes in guest language, reads a label, explains the 2026 Israeli region map and handles kosher/mevushal requests.
- **Likely confusion:** dry (no sugar) vs crisp (acidity); kosher vs mevushal; old five regions (taught in 02.8) vs the 2026 eight-region map.
- **Misconception:** "a region name means better wine". The 2026 map is explicitly not a quality ranking.
- **Expected hands-on result:** six rows; most Israeli labels kosher, fewer mevushal; most labels still show old region names in late 2026; lightest-to-fullest order puts crisp whites first and Cabernet/Syrah last. Cards: 10/12 or better.
- **Extension:** find one Israeli wine labelled with a new 2026 region or sub-region name and write the guest description.
- **Notes:** the 2026 map was confirmed only in the press (Montefiore/JPost, ice.co.il), not on gov.il as of 2026-10-17; recheck. Lesson 02.8 still describes the five legacy regions; it should get a one-line pointer to 07.3 (main session). Keep the Marawi and Golan material factual and neutral; the course takes no position on political questions.
