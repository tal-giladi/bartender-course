# Instructor notes — 01.1 What a bartender does and how this course trains you

- **Objective:** learner knows the roles, the honest scope of the course, and has a numeric free-pour baseline in a practice log.
- **Likely confusion:** barback vs bartender vs achmash. The achmash is a shift role, not a fixed job title; on a small bar it may be the head bartender.
- **Misconception:** "a course certificate gets me hired". Israeli bars hire on a trial shift (professional judgment); the course gives skill, not a credential.
- **Expected hands-on result:** free pours of 30 ml usually land 20–45 ml with a spread over 10 ml; 0–2 of 5 within ±5%. Anyone claiming 5/5 on the first try probably used the glass's markings; ask them to use a plain glass.
- **Extension:** repeat the baseline at 15 ml and 60 ml. Small volumes usually show larger percentage errors.
