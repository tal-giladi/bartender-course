# Instructor notes — 01.6 Ice: types, handling and why it matters

- **Objective:** learner picks ice by job, explains surface area and wet/dry ice, and handles ice as food, including burning the ice.
- **Likely confusion:** "colder ice = less dilution". Very cold dry ice can under-dilute a shaken drink; wet ice at 0 °C adds water faster. Chill and dilution go together.
- **Misconception:** a small piece of glass can be found and picked out. It cannot; burn the whole well.
- **Expected hands-on result:** crushed ice melts clearly more than cubes in 30 s (often 2× or more) and the water is colder; wet ice adds a few grams more than dry freezer ice. Numbers vary with freezer temperature; the direction matters.
- **Safety-critical:** scooping ice with a glass and leaving broken glass in the ice are safety-critical failures in the final assessment (outline §6).
- **Extension:** try directional freezing in a small open-top insulated box for 24–36 hours and compare clarity with tray ice.
- Module 3 (lesson 03.5 and the `dilution-lab` simulation) quantifies dilution; keep numbers here qualitative.
