# Instructor notes — 01.5 Glassware and when to use each glass

- **Objective:** learner picks a glass by service style (up, rocks, long) and working capacity, and handles and checks glass safely.
- **Likely confusion:** highball vs collins (overlapping capacities; collins is taller and narrower). Accept either for long drinks unless the spec names one.
- **Misconception:** "a bigger glass is more generous". A correct pour in too big a glass looks short and dilutes faster if filled with ice.
- **Expected hands-on result:** 5–8 glasses measured; working capacity 15–40 ml below brim; most homes lack a coupe, so a small wine glass stands in for drinks served up.
- **Safety-critical:** scooping ice with a glass is a safety-critical failure in the final assessment (outline §6).
- **Extension:** visit a bar's website or menu and list the glasses it uses for five drinks; compare with the table.
- **Note:** several capacities (coupe, Nick & Nora, wine, flute, copper mug, shot) are professional judgment; the shlish/chatzi beer sizes too.
