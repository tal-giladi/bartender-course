# Instructor notes — 01.4 Prep and garnish tools: juicers, peelers and knives

- **Objective:** learner preps wheels, wedges and peels safely and measures juice yield per lime.
- **Likely confusion:** "cut side down" in the hand press feels wrong to beginners; show a photo or demo if possible.
- **Misconception:** a blunt knife is safer. It is not: it needs more force and slips off citrus skin.
- **Expected hands-on result:** even wheels, eight similar wedges, peels with a thin pith layer; lime juice per fruit varies widely, so the learner's own number matters more than a textbook figure (Module 3 measures yield properly).
- **Safety-critical:** unsafe knife handling is a safety-critical failure in the final assessment (outline §6). Watch for: board without a damp cloth, flat fingers, knife in a sink.
- **Extension:** time 20 wedges and aim to cut the time by a third across three sessions without dropping the claw grip.
