# Instructor notes — 01.2 The bar station and your home practice station

- **Objective:** learner can name the parts of a station and has a mapped, reach-tested home station.
- **Likely confusion:** "well" vs "ice well" vs "well spirits". The well = ice well + speed rail; well spirits = house pours.
- **Misconception:** expensive tools make you better. Price matters far less than a jigger, a scale and fixed positions.
- **Expected hands-on result:** a station 60–80 cm wide, all items reachable without stepping; first setup 8–15 minutes, second faster from the map.
- **Extension:** visit a bar at a quiet hour (as a guest) and sketch its station from the customer side; compare with the diagram.
- **Note:** prices are professional judgment, not shop-checked; update if a price check is done later.
