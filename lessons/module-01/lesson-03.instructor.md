# Instructor notes — 01.3 Measuring, mixing and straining tools

- **Objective:** learner names the core tools, converts oz to ml (bar convention 30 ml), and hits ±5% with a jigger on 4 of 5 pours.
- **Likely confusion:** ¾ oz is written 20, 22.5 or 25 ml in different metric specs. Teach: follow the spec in front of you; this course's cards give the IBA ml figure.
- **Misconception:** "the brim is approximate". Under-filling by 1–3 ml is the most common jigger error.
- **Expected hands-on result:** 4–5 of 5 in tolerance at 30 and 45 ml; 15 ml is harder. Consistent 1–2 g under-pour = slow or partial tip.
- **Extension:** repeat the drill with sugar syrup (1:1 weighs about 1.23 g/ml, so convert before checking); this previews lesson 03.4.
- **Consistency:** barspoon 5 ml, dash 0.8 ml, 1 oz = 29.5735 ml match `simulations/common/bar-math.js`.
