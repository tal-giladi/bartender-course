# Instructor notes — 04.6 Blending, swizzling and throwing

- **Objective:** learner blends with a measured scoop of crushed ice and blender safety, swizzles until frost, and throws 4–5 times with spill under 5 g by the tenth water rep.
- **Likely confusion:** swizzling versus churning versus stirring with crushed ice. The palm-spin is the swizzle; up-and-down without spinning is churning.
- **Misconception:** "throwing is a flair trick". It is a working method for aeration and aroma, best with vermouth and sherry (Difford's).
- **Numbers:** Difford's aims for 30–35% dilution in 4–5 throws. Model `dilutionFraction('throw', 0.33, t)`: 5 s 0.25, 8 s 0.35, 10 s 0.39 (rough estimate per the bar-math comment).
- **Expected hands-on result:** spill falling from 15–30 g to under 5 g over ten reps; swizzle frost in 20–40 s; double-ice frozen drink stiffer and less sweet.
- **Safety:** blender blade and slippery floors. Hands and tools never in a running jug; dry the floor after water practice.
- **Extension:** throw and stir the same alcohol-free vermouth substitute (for example cold tea with a little grape juice and bitters-free aromatics) and compare texture side by side; later, optionally, a real Manhattan in Module 5.
