# Instructor notes — 04.2 Building drinks and highballs

- **Objective:** learner builds highballs in the correct order, keeps the fizz, pours within ±5% and builds rounds in passes.
- **Likely confusion:** "one lift" versus "stir". A lift is one stroke from the bottom up; built short drinks (Negroni, Old Fashioned) are stirred properly because they have no mixer.
- **Misconception:** less ice gives a "stronger" or "better value" drink. It gives a warmer, more diluted drink that looks short.
- **Expected hands-on result:** a 300 ml highball full of ice holds about 150–180 ml of liquid; single builds 20–30 s, round builds 12–18 s per drink; 8 of 10 tea pours in tolerance; the lifted drink clearly fizzier after 2 minutes.
- **Safety-critical:** scooping ice with a glass is a safety-critical failure (outline §6). Watch for it in the film.
- **Extension:** build a round of 8 mixed drinks (4 highballs, 2 spritzes, 2 built Negronis) in passes and target under 2 minutes total.
- **Link forward:** service sequencing in Module 8 builds on working in passes.
