# Instructor notes — 04.8 Technique drills: film, time and fix your movements

- **Objective:** module practical. Learner logs 10 reps × 8 movements, converts the evidence to rubric scores with the drill sheet, compares film with demonstrations and writes three specific fixes.
- **Rubric:** `templates/technique-rubric.md` is the same rubric used in 11.3 (practical technique, 25% of the final grade, 75% minimum). Free pour is optional here; score out of 18 if skipped.
- **Likely confusion:** spread (consistency) versus accuracy. Stir and shake reps are judged on spread around the learner's own average, not on hitting the model's cocktail numbers (water gains less; see 04.1).
- **Misconception:** a high total excuses a safety fault. It does not: ice scooped with a glass, broken glass in the ice or unsafe knife use fails the drill (outline §6).
- **Expected result:** first drill 55–80%; low lines usually measuring, stir spread and garnish speed. Re-test a week later typically raises the three weakest lines by about a point each.
- **Optional review:** if a working bartender can watch the film, ask them to score the rubric independently and compare; the course states self-scores are unverified.
- **Extension:** repeat the full drill with a 10% faster time target on build and garnish while keeping accuracy; or add ten thrown reps (04.6) with spill ≤ 5 g.
