# Instructor notes — 04.7 Garnishes: preparation and presentation

- **Objective:** learner cuts and expresses a twist correctly in ≤ 20 s, sets a consistent garnish tray in under 6 minutes, rims half a glass on the outside, and places all garnish without bare hands.
- **Likely confusion:** "twist" as a noun (the strip of peel) and as a verb (twisting the ends to express). Also drop vs mount vs discard: the spec decides.
- **Misconception:** garnish is decoration only. Expressed oils and salt rims change flavour; the cherry or coffee beans identify the drink.
- **Expected hands-on result:** twist times from 30–40 s down to about 20 s; visible oil sheen on water against a dark background; tray 6–8 minutes on a first try.
- **Safety-critical:** unsafe knife handling is a safety-critical failure in the final assessment. Bare-hand garnish is a hygiene fault, not safety-critical, but the rubric's garnish line requires a clean, consistent placement.
- **Excluded:** flamed zests (fire trick) are deliberately not taught; the lesson says why in one line.
- **Extension:** design a garnish map for a 6-drink menu (which garnish, which position, which glass) and test whether a friend can identify each drink by garnish alone.
