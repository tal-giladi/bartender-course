# Instructor notes — 06.2 White Lady, Sidecar and Pisco Sour

- **Objective:** recall IBA White Lady (40 gin / 30 triple sec / 20 lemon), Sidecar (50 cognac / 20 / 20) and Pisco Sour (60 pisco / 30 lemon / 20 syrup / 1 egg white, Amargo drops); liqueur as sweetener; safe foam.
- **Likely confusion:** White Lady vs Sidecar amounts; learners also mix in Difford's 50/22.5/22.5. Mnemonic: "Lady 40-30-20, Sidecar 50-20-20".
- **Misconception:** "every sour needs syrup" and "alcohol makes raw egg safe". Neither is true; the IBA triple-sec sours have no syrup, and pasteurised white or aquafaba is the course rule (see 04.4).
- **Expected hands-on result:** White Lady style sweeter than Sidecar style; reverse dry shake foam roughly 1–2 cm and stable at 5 minutes vs a few mm for a wet shake; bitters drops stay on a settled foam.
- **Extension:** have the learner make the Difford's White Lady balance (50/22.5/22.5 + 7.5 ml 2:1) in NA form with aquafaba and compare with the IBA balance.
- **Note:** egg white ≈ 30 ml and the Peru lime note are labelled professional judgment; pisco and Amargo Chuncho availability in Israel not verified.
