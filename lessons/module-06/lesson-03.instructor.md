# Instructor notes — 06.3 French 75, Tom Collins and Paloma

- **Objective:** recall IBA French 75 (30 gin / 15 lemon / 15 syrup / 60 Champagne), Tom Collins = IBA John Collins with Old Tom gin (45 / 30 / 15 / 60 soda, built on ice), IBA Paloma (50 tequila / 5 lime / pinch of salt / 100 pink grapefruit soda); top without foaming over.
- **Likely confusion:** the Tom Collins has no IBA page. The card cites the IBA John Collins page and its Old Tom note; this is stated on the card and in the recipe inbox.
- **Misconception:** "stir a fizzy drink well so it is mixed". One lift is enough; stirring loses CO2 (03.6). Also "salt makes it salty": a pinch reduces bitterness (03.3).
- **Expected hands-on result:** fast top foams to the rim; tilted slow top ≈ 1 cm foam and one pour; shaken Collins colder and more even at first; salted Paloma preferred blind.
- **Extension:** time a round of four French 75s with soda, aiming to top all four in one pass without a second pour.
- **Safety:** sparkling-wine cork warning; full opening procedure is in 07.4 (not linked yet because Module 7 is not written).
