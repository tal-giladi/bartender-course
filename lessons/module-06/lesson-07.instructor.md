# Instructor notes — 06.7 Israeli bar menus

- **Objective:** separate verified (named, dated source), professional judgment and unknown claims about Israeli menus; make the two arak serves from their named sources; survey three real menus and write findings sized to the sample.
- **Likely confusion:** a real source supporting a smaller claim (Saveur: "popular refresher on Tel Aviv beaches" ≠ "most popular drink in Israeli bars"). The claims-sort solution scores these as unknown.
- **Misconception:** "everyone knows it, so it is a fact". Bartender consensus is professional judgment; sales data is private.
- **Expected hands-on result:** claims sort 10–12/12; survey of ~25–40 drinks; sours and highballs usually dominate (judgment; the learner's sample decides). The CSV template has one example row marked "delete"; it is not real data.
- **Extension:** ask the learner to repeat the survey for one of the three bars in three months and note what changed.
- **Open items:** VAT-inclusive menu prices and any Israeli standard measure are not verified here (labelled); Module 10 and Module 9 should verify them on official sources. No official or industry sales data on Israeli cocktail preferences was found (2026-10-17).
- **Safety:** arak ABV warning before the optional tasting.
