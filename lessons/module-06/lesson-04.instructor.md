# Instructor notes — 06.4 Bloody Mary and Mai Tai

- **Objective:** recall IBA Bloody Mary (45 vodka / 90 tomato / 15 lemon / 2 dashes Worcestershire / Tabasco, celery salt, pepper to taste; stirred, rocks) and Mai Tai (30 + 30 rums / 15 curaçao / 15 orgeat / 30 lime / 7.5 syrup; shaken, double rocks or highball); season in steps; name allergens.
- **Likely confusion:** IBA Mai Tai "shake and pour" vs Difford's "strain over crushed ice". Both accepted; the card gives the IBA method first.
- **Misconception:** "a few drops of Worcestershire do not matter for a fish allergy" and "Mai Tai has pineapple juice and grenadine". Both wrong.
- **Expected hands-on result:** ladder shows each seasoning's role; shaken tomato drink froths and separates within ~2 minutes; learner writes a house heat scale (example 1/3/5 dashes, labelled professional judgment).
- **Extension:** blind-taste two Virgin Marys, one with celery salt and one with plain salt at the same weight, and describe the difference.
- **Safety:** no flame in tiki serves (stated); knife and crushed-ice caution; allergens (fish, barley, almond, celery). Worcestershire label verified on a UK product label PDF 2026-10-17; Israeli allergen law is Module 9.
