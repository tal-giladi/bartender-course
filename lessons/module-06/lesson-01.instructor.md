# Instructor notes — 06.1 Cocktail families

- **Objective:** sort any spec into one of the ten course families (the same enum as `curriculum/recipes.yaml`) with four questions; know the IBA sour range (base 40–60, citrus 15–30, sweetener close to citrus); see sour vs Collins vs fizz as one base, three serves.
- **Likely confusion:** drinks between families (Americano, Caipirinha, Mojito, French 75, Mai Tai). The solution file accepts a second family for these; insist on a reason, not the label.
- **Misconception:** "a family is the base spirit" (gin drinks, rum drinks). Show a Martini and a Tom Collins side by side: same base, nothing else in common.
- **Expected hands-on result:** 18–21/24 on the first sort; sour coldest and sharpest; Collins stays cold longest; fizz warms fastest (no ice in glass).
- **Extension:** give the learner three drinks from the IBA list not in this module (e.g. Bee's Knees, Tipperary, Jungle Bird) and ask for family, method and the one-line difference before they open the page.
- **Note:** the 60 : 22.5 : 22.5 starting sour ratio is labelled professional judgment; it happens to equal the IBA Gin Basil Smash.
