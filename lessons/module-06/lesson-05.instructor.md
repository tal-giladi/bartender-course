# Instructor notes — 06.5 More classics and modern classics

- **Objective:** recall eight specs by family and one-line difference: Gimlet (Difford's), Caipirinha, Cuba Libre, Penicillin, Paper Plane, Gin Basil Smash, Boulevardier (all IBA), Hugo Spritz (Difford's).
- **Likely confusion:** Boulevardier 45/30/30 vs Negroni 30/30/30; Penicillin's 22.5/22.5 and 7.5 float; which drinks are not IBA (Gimlet, Hugo). The IBA Gin Basil Smash says "pour into chilled cocktail glass"; the card recommends fine straining (labelled).
- **Misconception:** "a Gimlet is a gin sour with fresh lime and syrup". Classically it is gin and lime cordial (Difford's notes equal parts); fresh lime + syrup is a gin sour.
- **Expected hands-on result:** first recall 30–40/48; hard-muddled lime bitter; Hawthorne-only Smash has leaf bits; Cuba Libre build 20–30 s (target professional judgment).
- **Extension:** ask the learner to write the one-line difference for two IBA drinks not taught (e.g. Bramble, Last Word) and check against the IBA pages.
- **Note:** creation dates are Difford's Guide's; the Paper Plane's year differs between sources (2007/2008), stated in the lesson.
