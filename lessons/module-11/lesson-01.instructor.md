# Instructor notes — 11.1 How the final assessment works and is graded

- **Objective:** learners can compute the written blend and the weighted total, and apply the three pass conditions (total ≥ 80.0, every minimum, no safety-critical failure).
- **Grading contract:** course outline §6; `simulations/common/bar-math.js` `WEIGHTS`, `MINIMUMS`, `finalGrade`. Written = 0.50 × Module 11 quiz + 0.25 × lesson-quiz average (M1–10) + 0.25 × guest-scenarios exam (decision taken in the Module 11 plan to resolve the outline's "feeds the written component"). If a simulation computes the written score, it must use this blend.
- **Likely confusion:** that a high total compensates for a missed minimum (quiz q1, case 2).
- **Misconception:** that a safety failure fails the whole assessment and everything must be redone. It fails that part; only that part is remediated and retaken (q3).
- **Expected hands-on result:** case 1 total 79.0 (not passed), case 2 93.0 with service 66 (not passed), case 3 85.4 with a safety failure (not passed). Learners who pass any case have skipped a condition.
- **Honesty:** stress that everything is self-scored and unverified; the course is not a qualification. Encourage a working bartender's review of the 11.3 film.
- **Extension:** ask the learner which single retake raises case 1 above 80 most cheaply (recall, because of its 25% weight).
