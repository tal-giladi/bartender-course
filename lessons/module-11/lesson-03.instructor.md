# Instructor notes — 11.3 Practical technique exam

- **Objective:** two filmed rounds of a fixed exam set (home-practice ingredients), scored on `templates/technique-rubric.md` from evidence; minimum 75% (15/20 or 14/18); safety-critical check separate from the score.
- **Evidence rules:** `labs/module-11/technique-exam-sheet.md` — measuring 8 pours (2 = 8/8, 1 = 6–7); movement lines 2 = both rounds, 1 = one; reset 2 = both ≤ 90 s; safety 2/1/0 for minor faults. Same standards as 04.8 and the drill sheet.
- **Reviewer:** optional `labs/module-11/reviewer-form.md`; record the lower score. Be explicit that a review is informal and not a certification.
- **Likely confusion:** a safety-critical failure vs a minor safety fault (an uncapped bottle costs a point; a glass in the ice fails the part).
- **Misconception:** stopping the camera to retry is fine. It is not; recovery is assessed.
- **Expected result:** 70–90% (professional judgment); 15 ml pours and twist speed are the common low lines.
- **Extension:** run the exam set with a timer target of 12 minutes per round to add speed pressure; compare accuracy with the untimed version.
