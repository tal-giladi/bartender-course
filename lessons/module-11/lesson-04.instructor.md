# Instructor notes — 11.4 Simulated service exam

- **Objective:** one complete `service-sim?preset=exam` run under exam conditions; service score ≥ 70; numeric debrief.
- **Simulation contract (`exam`):** 20 tickets, 1–4 drinks incl. rounds of 3–4; menu = 12 Module 5 drinks + White Lady, Sidecar, Pisco Sour, French 75, Tom Collins, Paloma, Bloody Mary, Mai Tai + draught beer + house wine; waves 6 (min 0–3), 6 (3–10), 8 (10–18); one seeded stock-out (lime juice, Aperol or ginger beer) around ticket 8; one correct drink sent back (remake = waste, not error); hints off; recipe cards hidden; same correctness, accuracy, waste, ticket-time and dead-drink rules as 08.5/08.8. Score = accuracy − time penalty ((avg − 300) ÷ 15 above 300 s, cap 20) − waste penalty ((waste − 5) × 2 above 5%, cap 20), floor 0, rounded. End screen: three metrics, both penalties, score, "copy results" block with seed.
- **Likely confusion:** that the formula rewards speed. It rewards accuracy first (quiz q2).
- **Misconception:** stock-out = unfair run, restart. No restarts; early barback requests are the skill.
- **Expected result:** 85–92% accuracy, 280–340 s, score high 70s to low 90s after 08.8 (professional judgment).
- **Extension:** retake with the same seed after the debrief (outside exam conditions) to isolate the effect of the one change.
