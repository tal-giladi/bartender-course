# Instructor notes — 11.7 Your final performance report

- **Objective:** module and course practical piece: fill `templates/performance-report.md` from `labs/module-11/exam-score-sheet.md`; decide the result with the three conditions; three evidence-based strengths and areas; dated remediation plan; honest CV line; next steps.
- **Worked example:** `labs/module-11/performance-report-example-solution.md` (Dana, total 80.6, one service retake). It matches the 11.1 worked example.
- **Likely confusion:** that a missed minimum or a safety failure makes the report pointless. It turns the report into a precise plan for one retake.
- **Misconception:** describing the course as a certificate or diploma (quiz q2). The course is not accredited and grants no licence; scores are self-assessed.
- **Expected result:** a two-page report; strengths usually in recall and Module 4 movements, areas in guest conversations and first-wave sequencing.
- **Honesty:** encourage a working bartender to read the report and watch the 11.3 film; their additions are usually the most useful line in it.
- **Extension:** ask the learner to role-play the "what can you do?" conversation with a friend, using only facts from the report, in under one minute.
