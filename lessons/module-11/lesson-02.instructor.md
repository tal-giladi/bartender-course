# Instructor notes — 11.2 Recipe recall exam

- **Objective:** score at least 80% (64 / 80) on 20 random cards from the 30 specs in `curriculum/recipes.yaml`; mark strictly; plan remediation from the error pattern.
- **Simulation contract (`recipe-drill?preset=exam`):** 20 cards without repeats from all of `recipes.js`; inputs for every ingredient and ml, method, glass and ice, garnish; 4 points per card exactly as 05.8 (ml ±5 ml; dashes, sprigs, cubes, bar spoons, "to top" as the spec); no hints and no spec view until the end; score = points ÷ 80 × 100; end screen with per-card breakdown, the spec of each card, and a "copy results" block (seed, date, points, %). `&seed=` fixes the draw.
- **Paper fallback:** `labs/module-11/recall-exam-grid.md` (numbered list of 30, 20-row grid, marking rules, error log). Answer key = `references/recipe-library.md`.
- **Likely confusion:** variations vs the IBA spec. Only variations the course card names as accepted alternatives score.
- **Misconception:** "close enough" ml. Quiz q1.
- **Expected result:** 70–90% on a first attempt after Modules 5–6 recall practice (professional judgment); modifier ml and late-learned garnishes are the common misses.
- **Extension:** have the learner sit the paper version with a different draw and compare; a gap above 10 points suggests the simulation input format is helping or hurting recall.
