# Instructor notes — 05.1 How to read, learn and remember a cocktail spec

- **Objective:** learner converts an IBA spec into the course card, sorts the twelve drinks into four templates, and runs a scored Leitner round.
- **Likely confusion:** "chilled cocktail glass" on IBA pages. It means a stemmed glass served up (coupe or martini glass), no ice.
- **Misconception:** "there is one correct spec". IBA, Difford's and house specs differ; the course teaches IBA as default, variations by source, and house spec at work.
- **Expected hands-on result:** 2–4 of 12 cards pass on day 1; misses cluster on small-ingredient ml, garnish and method.
- **Extension:** have the learner write the IBA Dry Martini and Difford's 4:1 Martini on two separate cards and explain in one line why they are not merged.
- **Sources caveat:** memory research is summarised from Wikipedia overview articles (testing effect, spaced repetition); intervals given (daily, 2–3 days, weekly) are a simple Leitner schedule, not a proven optimum.
