# Instructor notes — 05.4 Daiquiri and Margarita

- **Objective:** recall IBA Daiquiri (60 white rum / 20 lime / 2 bar spoons superfine sugar) and Margarita (50 agave tequila / 20 triple sec / 15 lime, half salt rim optional); shake and double strain; convert sugar to syrup; rim half the outside.
- **Likely confusion:** the course outline's sample card uses 15 ml 1:1 syrup; the IBA uses sugar. Both are taught: IBA on the card, syrup as the substitution (≈ 8 g sugar ≈ 15 ml 1:1 ≈ 10 ml 2:1, professional judgment).
- **Misconception:** "Margaritas have equal tequila, lime and triple sec" or "more lime is more authentic". The IBA spec has less lime than triple sec; the 1937 Picador is 2:1:1.
- **Expected hands-on result:** sugar dissolves in 20–40 s; sugar and syrup versions taste nearly the same; water-based shake gains about 20–30% in 12 s (consistent with 03.5); half rim 20–30 s with practice.
- **Extension:** have the learner make the Difford's Daiquiri balance (50/15/10 of 2:1) in the NA version and compare it with the IBA balance.
- **Safety:** shaker seal warning; knife caution; alcohol-tasting caution.
