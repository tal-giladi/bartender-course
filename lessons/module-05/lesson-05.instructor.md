# Instructor notes — 05.5 Whiskey Sour and Cosmopolitan

- **Objective:** recall IBA Whiskey Sour (45 bourbon / 25 lemon / 20 syrup / few drops egg white optional) and Cosmopolitan (40 citrus vodka / 15 Cointreau / 15 lime / 30 cranberry); foam safely with dry or reverse dry shake; disclose egg.
- **Likely confusion:** IBA "sugar syrup" has no ratio stated; the course reads it as 1:1 and gives the 2:1 swap.
- **Misconception:** "fresh eggs are safe raw". FDA: Salmonella can be in clean, uncracked eggs; use pasteurised egg white or aquafaba.
- **Expected hands-on result:** aquafaba foam about 1–2 cm, reverse dry slightly thicker in most kits; Cosmopolitan-style volume about 120–130 ml after the shake; pale pink colour.
- **Extension:** compare 15 ml and 30 ml aquafaba for foam height and taste (too much tastes of chickpea).
- **Safety:** raw-egg warning (FDA), allergen disclosure, shaker-without-ice warning, no flaming garnishes (DeGroff's flamed zest replaced by expressing).
