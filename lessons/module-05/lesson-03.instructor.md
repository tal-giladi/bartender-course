# Instructor notes — 05.3 Old Fashioned and Negroni

- **Objective:** recall IBA Old Fashioned (45 ml bourbon or rye, sugar cube, few dashes Angostura and water, muddle, ice, stir; orange and cherry) and Negroni (30/30/30 built on ice, half orange slice); dissolve sugar before ice; build rounds efficiently.
- **Likely confusion:** "few dashes" as a fixed number. IBA leaves it open; 2–3 is common practice (professional judgment); the house spec decides.
- **Misconception:** "a proper Old Fashioned has muddled orange and cherry". Wikipedia dates fruit garnishes to around 1930 and widespread fruit muddling much later; IBA garnishes with fruit but does not muddle it.
- **Expected hands-on result:** cube muddling 30–60 s; syrup versions about half the time; four-Negroni round under 2 minutes by the second try; measured volume close to 90 ml.
- **Extension:** compare 10 ml 1:1 and 7.5 ml 2:1 syrup in the tea Old Fashioned blind (link to the 03.4 swap rule).
- **Safety:** muddle only in thick glass; knife caution; alcohol-tasting caution.
