# Instructor notes — 05.8 Recipe recall and a twelve-drink practice session

- **Objective:** blind recall of all twelve IBA specs scored 4 points per drink (ml ±5, method, glass and ice, garnish; pass 39/48 = 80%, same standard as 11.2); one planned twelve-drink home session, logged; review plan for the weakest three.
- **Likely confusion:** "±5 ml" applied to dashes, cubes and sprigs. Those are scored as written in the spec; the tolerance is for ml amounts.
- **Misconception:** "the session is about speed". The log is mainly for spec errors and order; most time goes on prep, washing and ice, which is the lesson for Module 8.
- **Expected hands-on result:** after a week of Leitner reviews, 36–44/48; building totals 15–25 minutes, the whole session about 60 minutes plus prep and reset; volumes within about 10%.
- **Extension:** repeat the session a week later with the drinks drawn in random order (interleaved) and compare times.
- **Safety:** knives, glass, hot coffee, aquafaba/pasteurised egg only; the session is alcohol-free; any real tasting kept to small sips, ≤ 1 standard drink, never before driving.
