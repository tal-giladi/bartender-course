# Instructor notes — 05.2 Martini and Manhattan

- **Objective:** recall IBA Dry Martini (60 gin / 10 dry vermouth, lemon oil or olive on request) and Manhattan (50 rye / 20 sweet vermouth / 1 dash Angostura, cherry); stir and strain consistently; name dry/wet and rye/bourbon variations with sources.
- **Likely confusion:** "dry" read as "less sweet in general". In a Martini it only means less vermouth.
- **Misconception:** "James Bond style shaken is the proper Martini". Shaking clouds and over-dilutes a spirit-only drink; stirring is the standard method (IBA).
- **Expected hands-on result:** water drills show small, consistent gains (about 8–14 g with fresh ice) and 60–75 s per rep; consistency between reps matters more than the absolute number.
- **Extension:** compare a stirred and a shaken water/tea rep side by side for clarity and weight gain.
- **Safety:** alcohol-tasting caution (half size, sip and spit, never before driving); peeler caution.
