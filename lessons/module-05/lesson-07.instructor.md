# Instructor notes — 05.7 Aperol Spritz and Espresso Martini

- **Objective:** recall IBA Spritz (90 Prosecco / 60 Aperol / splash soda, wine glass on ice, orange slice) and Espresso Martini (50 vodka / 30 Kahlúa / 10 syrup / 1 strong espresso, shaken, 3 coffee beans); build in order; foam from fresh espresso; caffeine message.
- **Likely confusion:** IBA calls it "Spritz"; guests say "Aperol Spritz". IBA soda is "a splash"; Aperol's site gives 30 ml (3-2-1). Aperol's Ireland page also shows 75/50/25.
- **Misconception:** "an Espresso Martini sobers you up". CDC: caffeine does not reduce alcohol's effects.
- **Expected hands-on result:** correct-order spritz even in colour after one stir; fresh-coffee foam about 5–10 mm, old coffee almost none, 4-second shake thin and short-lived.
- **Extension:** compare IBA (with 10 ml syrup) and Difford's (no syrup, saline) balance in the alcohol-free version.
- **Sources caveat:** the "about 30 ml" espresso shot is professional judgment; IBA gives no volume. Prosecco keeping-time is professional judgment.
- **Safety:** hot espresso caution in the shaker; caffeine-and-alcohol warning (CDC).
