# Instructor notes — 02.5 Arak and other regional spirits

- **Objective:** learner serves arak correctly (order and ratio), explains the louche, and places other regional spirits.
- **Likely confusion:** arak vs sambuca (dry spirit vs sweet liqueur); raki vs rakia (Balkan fruit brandy, not anise).
- **Misconception:** "cloudy arak is spoiled"; "add ice first to chill it faster".
- **Expected hands-on result:** water pours start 10–20 ml off target, reaching ±5–10 ml by the fifth pour. Optional demo: water glass evenly milky; ice-first glass may show flakes or film.
- **Extension:** price check (no buying needed) of three arak bottles in a local shop: note ABV, producer, grape or not if stated, and the Hebrew label warning (preview of 02.9).
- **Safety:** the flaming warning is deliberate; never demonstrate fire with anise spirits.
