# Instructor notes — 02.9 Labels, storage and substitutions: classify a back bar

- **Objective:** module practical. Learner classifies 30 products (≥80%), names 20 beverages from descriptions (≥16/20 in under 6 min), and writes guest-facing aroma descriptions.
- **Likely confusion:** the 15.5% warning threshold (vermouths at 16–18% are "strong"); spirit drink vs rum under 37.5%; mixto reposado.
- **Misconception:** "the bartender is legally responsible for the label warning". The statutory duty concerns selling containers without the warning; checking stock is good practice, not the bartender's legal role (professional judgment; Module 9 covers duties that are personal).
- **Expected hands-on result:** back bar 20–26 first try; drill 14–18 in 5–8 minutes; aroma blind scores equal or higher than in the original lessons.
- **Extension:** take photos (with permission) of 10 real back labels in a shop and run the 30-second label routine on each; compare with a classmate or a working bartender.
- **Data note:** the 30 products are fictional descriptions to avoid stating facts about real brands; this is stated in the lab README and the solution.
