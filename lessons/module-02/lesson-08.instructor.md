# Instructor notes — 02.8 Beer, cider, wine and alcohol-free drinks at a glance

- **Objective:** learner classifies beer, cider, wine and NA products and reads ABV and alcohol-free claims exactly.
- **Likely confusion:** brut vs extra dry; cider (alcoholic) vs סיידר as sweet apple juice; "lager = light".
- **Misconception:** "alcohol-free means 0%". Several NA beers are 0.3–0.5%.
- **Expected hands-on result:** 20 rows; beers clustered at 4–6%, wines at 11–15%; at least one 0.0% and one >0% "alcohol-free" product; both Hebrew warning wordings found (strong vs other drinks), which sets up 02.9.
- **Extension:** for each of three alcohol-free products, write a one-line guest description that states the exact ABV.
- **Note:** Israeli wine region details are from a 2013 article; check for an official update before expanding in Module 7.
