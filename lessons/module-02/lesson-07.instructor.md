# Instructor notes — 02.7 Vermouth, fortified wines and aperitifs

- **Objective:** learner picks the correct vermouth, knows the main sherry and port styles, and stores wine-based products correctly.
- **Likely confusion:** bianco (pale but sweet) vs dry vermouth; bitters vs aperitivo bitters; fino vs oloroso.
- **Misconception:** "vermouth is a spirit and keeps forever"; "sherry is always sweet".
- **Expected hands-on result:** open apple browns within 1 hour; after 48 h the open warm juice is darker and sourer, sometimes fizzy, while the sealed fridge jar is close to fresh. Learners should connect this to dating and refrigerating vermouth.
- **Extension:** draft a one-page "opened bottle" policy for a small bar (what gets dated, where it is stored, how long), and compare with a working bar's practice if possible.
