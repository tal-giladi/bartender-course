# Instructor notes — 02.3 Rum, tequila and mezcal

- **Objective:** learner reads rum, tequila and mezcal labels correctly and substitutes within style.
- **Likely confusion:** "gold" tequila vs aged classes; tequila as a kind of mezcal (historically related, now legally separate).
- **Misconception:** "all mezcal is smoky" and "all tequila is 100% agave".
- **Expected hands-on result:** sweeteners ranked light to dark with caramel/toffee/bitter notes increasing; molasses perceived as less sweet than expected; label drill 6–8/8, most common error on label 2 (mixto) and label 5 (spiced rum below 37.5%).
- **Extension:** visit a shop and photograph five agave-spirit labels; classify each with the 20-second label routine.
