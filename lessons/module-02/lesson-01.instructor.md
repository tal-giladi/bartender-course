# Instructor notes — 02.1 How alcohol is made: fermentation, distillation, maturation and ABV

- **Objective:** learner can explain the three production steps and do ml → grams → standard drinks and mixed-drink ABV without help.
- **Likely confusion:** ml of alcohol vs grams of alcohol (forgetting × 0.789); share of spirit in a drink vs share of alcohol.
- **Misconception:** "dark spirits are stronger" or "dark means old". Colour comes from wood and sometimes caramel; ABV is on the label.
- **Expected hands-on result:** sugar bottle balloon visibly lifts in 15–30 min; control bottle barely moves. Worksheet within ±0.1 g of the solution.
- **Extension:** take the learner's own house pour (e.g. 50 ml) and build a one-page "standard drinks per serve" card for every pour size on a real menu.
- **Safety:** reinforce that home distillation is never part of the course.
