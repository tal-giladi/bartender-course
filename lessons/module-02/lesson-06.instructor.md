# Instructor notes — 02.6 Liqueurs and bitters

- **Objective:** learner defines liqueurs, sorts them by family, handles bitters for alcohol-avoiding guests and understands density for layering.
- **Likely confusion:** bitters vs bitter liqueurs (aperitivo, amaro). Bitters are dashed; bitter liqueurs are poured in ml (02.7).
- **Misconception:** "a dash of bitters is alcohol-free". Use the 0.36% ABV example; the issue is the guest's choice, not the number.
- **Expected hands-on result:** water/tea ≈ 50 g, simple syrup ≈ 61–62 g, rich syrup ≈ 66–67 g per 50 ml; three layers visible with a slow spoon pour. Off-by-several-grams results usually mean the jigger was not filled exactly or the syrup was not fully dissolved.
- **Extension:** find the ABV and storage advice on three liqueur labels at a shop; note any allergen statements.
