# Instructor notes — 02.4 Whisky and brandy

- **Objective:** learner knows Scotch and bourbon minimums, cognac age labels, and can describe styles by aroma for guests.
- **Likely confusion:** age statement = youngest whisky, not average; XO vs Napoléon after the 2018 change.
- **Misconception:** "bourbon must be from Kentucky"; "smooth" as a style. Push learners to describe flavours.
- **Expected hands-on result:** blind round 1 about 4–5/7, round 2 one or two better; clove/cinnamon and bread/dried fruit most confused.
- **Extension:** read the back labels of three whiskies in a shop and classify each into country, style and Scotch category where relevant.
