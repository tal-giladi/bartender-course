# Instructor notes — 09.3 Allergens and ingredient questions

- **Objective:** the 14 allergens (EU 1169/2011 Annex II, adopted in Israel) plus Israel's special fava rule; Israeli legal status (packaged: highlighted allergens; non-prepacked: deferred to 1.1.2032); the allergy routine; clean set; anaphylaxis first actions (call 101).
- **Likely confusion:** "not required by law" vs "allowed to guess". The deferral means no statutory label duty for mixed drinks yet; it does not change the safety duty a bar takes on when it serves food and drink.
- **Misconception:** "spirits are distilled so they are allergen-free". The distillation exemption covers the base spirit; liqueurs, syrups, cream and egg added afterwards are not exempt, and many bottles above 1.2% ABV have no ingredient list.
- **Expected hands-on result:** learners find egg and almond; most miss celery salt, Worcestershire (fish, often barley malt vinegar), sulphites in vermouth and sparkling, and treat a silent liqueur label as "safe". Clean set at home 1–2 minutes.
- **Extension:** ask the learner to write the allergen card for their 06.8 menu (each drink, its allergens and the bottles to check).
- **Safety-critical:** ignoring a declared allergy or guessing ingredients is a safety-critical failure in 11.5; the `allergy` simulation flags guessing, orgeat and an unwashed shaker.
- **First aid:** the lesson gives first actions only and recommends a certified first-aid course; do not expand into medical instruction.
