# Instructor notes — 09.7 Guest scenarios: seven situations, practised

- **Objective:** module practical. Seven `guest-scenarios` presets (`underage`, `intoxicated`, `unclear`, `allergy`, `complaint`, `strong`, `alcohol-free`), each with a written response scored on the five-criterion rubric (`labs/module-09/scenario-rubric.md`), then a rerun of the two weakest.
- **Scoring contract (for the simulation build):** 4 decision points × 3 choices; 2/1/0 points; some 0-choices flagged safety-critical; pass = ≥ 6/8 and no flag. `exam` (11.5) = all seven in random order. Full table in `curriculum/module-09-plan.md`.
- **Likely confusion:** points vs safety flags. A high score with a flag is a fail.
- **Misconception:** "alcohol-free means no spirit". Bitters and 0.5% products carry alcohol; say the ABV or leave them out (02.6, 07.5).
- **Expected hands-on result:** complaint and unclear pass first time; typical first-run flags are the phone-photo ID, the friend's beer passed to the minor, the unwashed Mai Tai shaker and bitters in the alcohol-free drink.
- **Extension:** partner role-play where the guest argues back; or mix two situations (an intoxicated guest who also declares an allergy).
- **Honesty:** self-scoring is unverified; recommend review by a working bartender where possible.
- **Link forward:** 11.5 repeats all seven as an exam and treats the safety items as pass/fail.
