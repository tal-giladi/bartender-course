# Instructor notes — 09.1 Greeting guests, taking orders and recommending

- **Objective:** acknowledge within ~10 s, greet within ~1 min; five-part order (number, drink, modifier, special need, who/where) with check-back; two questions + two options; close the visit (bill, payment in front of the guest, goodbye).
- **Likely confusion:** acknowledging vs greeting. Acknowledging is a one-second signal while busy; greeting starts the conversation when free.
- **Misconception:** "checking back sounds like I wasn't listening". Guests experience it as care when it is phrased warmly ("So that's…"), and it prevents remakes.
- **Expected hands-on result:** 1–2 modifiers caught by check-back across ten orders; 10/10 after check-back is the target. Orders 2 (self-correction) and 5 (three-way split) are the usual misses. Order 8 must be refused and redirected (link to 09.3); order 9 must lead to alcohol-free options.
- **Extension:** have the partner order in Hebrew for half the cards, or play two guests interrupting each other.
- **Link back/forward:** 07.6 (recommendation routine), 08.7 (check-back with the team); 09.2 (unclear orders), 09.3 (allergy), 09.4 (state), 09.5 (ID).
