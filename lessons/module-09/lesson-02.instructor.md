# Instructor notes — 09.2 Complaints, substitutions and unclear orders

- **Objective:** listen-apologise-solve-thank; adjust vs remake vs replace; the manager owns refunds and comps; honest substitutions told before pouring; clarify contradictory or ambiguous orders with two concrete options.
- **Likely confusion:** apologising is not admitting the spec is wrong. You apologise for the guest's experience.
- **Misconception:** "a free drink fixes any complaint". Comps are the manager's decision; a quick, correct remake usually fixes more.
- **Expected hands-on result:** first drafts often defend the recipe (case 1) or promise free drinks (cases 4 and 8). The adjustment drill shows that a 5 ml change is clearly noticeable in a short drink. Simulation target ≥ 6/8 in `complaint` and `unclear`.
- **Extension:** role-play case 8 with the partner getting louder; the learner practises lowering their voice and calling the manager.
- **Safety links:** case 3 (glass chip) connects to burning the ice (01.6); case 4 connects to 09.4 (offer a double only to a sober guest).
- **Simulation note:** `complaint` and `unclear` have no safety-critical choices; they are scored on the steps.
