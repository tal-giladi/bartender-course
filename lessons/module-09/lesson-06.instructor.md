# Instructor notes — 09.6 Food safety and workplace safety

- **Objective:** Ministry of Health licence conditions (item 4.2A: +5 °C, −18 °C, +65 °C, recording thermometer and three-month records, worker hygiene, illness and wounds, trained staff); cross-contamination routes; the employer's training duty (1999 regulations, 2026 amendment in force 16 October 2026); incident and near-miss reporting; guest safety.
- **Likely confusion:** licence conditions vs statute. The MoH numbers here are conditions in the uniform specification of a licensing item, binding on that licensed business; a pure pub (item 4.8) may carry different conditions. Learners should ask their venue.
- **Misconception:** "gloves make it OK to work sick or with a cut". The conditions exclude infectious workers and open wounds from food work; small covered cuts with a glove are a professional-judgment compromise.
- **Expected hands-on result:** typical fails are chemicals near ice or glass, unlabelled bottles, jewellery and no hand-washing routine. Home fridges often read above 5 °C on upper shelves.
- **Extension:** ask the learner to write a one-page opening food-safety checklist for a real bar job, combining 08.1 and this lesson.
- **Currency:** the training-regulations amendment came into force on 16 October 2026; uniform-specification copies are undated. Re-check before each course run.
