# Instructor notes — 09.5 Israeli law: age verification, alcohol sale and licensing

- **Objective:** Penal Law s. 193A (minors: (a), (a1), (a2), (b), (c) ID power, (c1) presumption, (d)–(e) sign) and s. 193(c) (drunk persons); ID check and cut-off date; 23:00–06:00 ban and its on-premises exemption; licensing items 4.8 / 4.2 and the police licence conditions; police closure powers (s. 23); Smoking Law duties; four kinds of rules.
- **Research date:** 2026-10-18. Nevo consolidated texts: Penal Law (to 2 July 2026), Licensing of Businesses Law (to 21 July 2026, includes the wine-in-large-stores exception), Smoking Law (to 8 Dec 2023). Police uniform specification copies on gov.il are undated: re-check before each course run.
- **Likely confusion:** the law gives a *power* to ask for ID (s. 193A(c)); the licence condition makes ID a requirement for anyone who wants to drink in a licensed bar; the presumption (c1) makes ID the practical defence.
- **Misconception:** "a parent can let their teenager drink at the bar". The family exception is in (a2) (supply in public places), not in (b) (businesses serving on the premises).
- **Misconception:** "the 23:00 law closes bars". Bars are exempt for drinking on the premises; takeaway sales are not.
- **Open points (do not overstate):** current status of the 2010 seizure powers (enacted as a temporary provision) not confirmed; whether the Penal Law's "intoxicating drink" uses the 2% line not confirmed; no statutory list of acceptable ID documents; no national bar closing time found. The State Comptroller (2016) also reports that one Magistrates' Court held the presumption unconstitutional in a specific case; the provision remains in the consolidated law. Mention only if a learner asks.
- **Expected hands-on result:** misses on cases 1, 3 and 2; ID cards B, I and J are the traps.
- **Extension:** have the learner find the minors sign and occupancy sign at a real venue and compare them with the spec.
- **Safety-critical:** serving or recommending alcohol to a possible minor without an ID check (11.1, 11.5); `underage` flags it.
