# Instructor notes — 03.6 Carbonation and how to keep it

- **Objective:** learner explains Henry's law in bar terms (pressure, cold), nucleation and agitation, builds a fizzy highball, and notices carbonation's tartness.
- **Likely confusion:** "bubbles are the gas being made". The gas was dissolved under pressure; bubbles are it leaving.
- **Misconception:** "more stirring mixes a highball better". One gentle lift is enough; pre-mix non-soda ingredients if there are several (Garret Richard / Punch, after Dave Arnold).
- **Expected hands-on result:** careful build stays lively at 10 min; careless build foams then goes flat and watery by 5 min; weight loss difference small (1–3 g), so taste is the main evidence. Soda version of a mix tastes sharper than still.
- **Measurement note:** wipe condensation before weighing; melted ice does not change mass, so weight loss ≈ CO2 escaped (plus a little evaporation).
- **Extension:** reopened 1.5 L bottle tasted on day 0, 1 and 2 against a fresh small bottle, to justify small bottles for low-volume mixers.
- **Safety:** CO2 pressure warning; no dry ice; never shake carbonated ingredients.
