# Instructor notes — 03.7 Diagnose and fix unbalanced drinks (module practical)

- **Objective:** learner tastes in a fixed order, names one of six faults, chooses rescue vs remake, and fixes at least 5 of 6 blind NA drinks, with weights and dilution % as evidence.
- **Likely confusion:** under-diluted (warm) read as "too sweet"; over-diluted read as "not enough syrup"; bitter/astringent read as "too sour".
- **Misconception:** "every fault can be rescued". Removal faults (over-diluted, too sweet beyond a small margin) need a remake; rescues change volume and spec.
- **Expected hands-on result:** 5–6 of 6 named; card C dilution 3–8%, card D 45–60% vs reference about 20–30%; saline improves card F but the remake with 4-minute tea is the real fix.
- **Extension:** partner creates a seventh card with two faults at once (e.g. too sweet and under-diluted); the learner must fix them in the right order.
- **Self-scoring:** results are self-assessed and unverified; the scoring sheet says so.
- **Uses:** templates/dilution-worksheet.md (balance diagnosis table), labs/module-03/unbalanced-drinks.md and -solution.md.
