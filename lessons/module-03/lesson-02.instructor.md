# Instructor notes — 03.2 Measuring and pouring accurately

- **Objective:** learner computes error %, checks syrup by weight with density, and calibrates a free-pour count to hit 30 ml within ±5% on 3 of 5 pours.
- **Likely confusion:** tolerance as a fixed ±1 g or ±3 ml rather than a percentage; ±1 g is used only at 15 ml because of scale resolution.
- **Misconception:** "a 4 count is 30 ml". It is a personal rhythm that depends on pourer, liquid and temperature; it must be calibrated and re-checked.
- **Expected hands-on result:** 2–3 of 5 free pours in at 30 ml on day one, improving with practice; syrup jigger 1–2 g under target with a normal tip, more with a slow tip.
- **Extension:** repeat the free-pour drill with 1:1 syrup in the pourer bottle and find the count for 15 ml; it is slower than water.
- **Consistency:** ±5% and `accuracyError`/`withinTolerance` match `simulations/common/bar-math.js` and the 11.3 technique rubric. Densities: 1:1 = 1.23 g/ml (Engineering ToolBox 50% sucrose 1.2295), 2:1 ≈ 1.33 (interpolated 60–70%).
