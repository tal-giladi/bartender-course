# Station checklist

Use it for every setup and reset drill. Tick each item and note the time when you finish each phase.

## Setup

- [ ] Hands washed, hair tied back, apron or towel on
- [ ] Work surface wiped and sanitised; cutting board on a damp cloth so it cannot slide
- [ ] Ice well (or bowl) filled; ice scoop in its holder, never left in the ice
- [ ] Tools out in a fixed order: shaker tins, jiggers, bar spoon, strainers, muddler, peeler, knife
- [ ] Speed rail (or a tray) with bottles, labels facing out, most-used nearest your hand
- [ ] Juices and syrups labelled with name and date, chilled
- [ ] Garnishes cut and covered
- [ ] Glasses clean, polished and checked for chips; chipped glass removed
- [ ] Bar towels: one dry for glass, one damp for the surface
- [ ] Bin and a container for empties within reach
- [ ] Time at end of setup: ____

## Reset (after each round)

- [ ] Tins and jiggers rinsed and back in place
- [ ] Spills wiped, surface dry
- [ ] Bottles capped and back in their spot
- [ ] Used glasses cleared
- [ ] Time at end of reset: ____

## Close

- [ ] Perishables labelled, covered and refrigerated or discarded
- [ ] Ice well emptied and dried
- [ ] Tools washed, sanitised and air-dried
- [ ] Surfaces cleaned; floor checked for glass and spills
- [ ] Time at end of close: ____
