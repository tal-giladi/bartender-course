# Bartending: From Zero to Your First Bar Job in Israel

Learn to work behind a bar — tools, spirits, technique, the classic cocktails, speed, costing and responsible service under Israeli law — by practising at home with water, inexpensive equipment, alcohol-free experiments and interactive simulations.

This is a self-paced course for adults (18+) with no bar experience. It has 11 modules and 80 lessons, about 55–65 hours over 8–10 weeks. Every lesson ends in something you do: a water drill, an alcohol-free mixing experiment, a simulation run with a target, a calculator exercise, a filmed self-assessment or a scripted role-play. Module 11 is a graded final assessment that you score yourself with clear rubrics.

## What you will learn

- Set up and reset a bar station and use every common tool, glass and type of ice correctly.
- Know the main spirits, liqueurs, wines and beers, read labels and make sensible substitutions.
- Measure accurately, scale recipes and control dilution, temperature and balance.
- Build, stir, shake, strain, muddle, layer and garnish cleanly and consistently.
- Make 30+ classic cocktails from memory and understand the families behind them.
- Work fast under pressure: sequence orders, batch, manage stock and keep the station clean.
- Serve responsibly: check age, handle intoxication, allergies and complaints, within Israeli law.
- Cost drinks, set menu prices and reconcile inventory.

## How to use it

Start with [Module 1](lessons/module-01/lesson-01.md) and work in order. The [course map](COURSE-MAP.md) shows every module, its practical piece and the simulations it uses. Keep a [practice log](templates/practice-log.md) from day one. The [recipe library](references/recipe-library.md) and [Hebrew at the bar](references/hebrew-bar-terms.md) are reference pages you can return to any time.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> This course is educational. It is not an accredited qualification and grants no licence or certificate. Practise mostly with water and alcohol-free ingredients; if you taste real alcohol, keep it small, optional and never before driving. Follow Israeli law on the sale and service of alcohol.
