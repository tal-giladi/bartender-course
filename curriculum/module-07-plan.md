# Module 07 plan — Beer, Wine, Sparkling Drinks and Beverage Service

Planning file, not imported. Written 2026-10-17.

Module practical piece: 07.6 recommendation role-play — 10 scripted guests, the learner writes a recommendation for each from a sample drinks list, then compares with model answers and self-scores with a rubric. Files: `labs/module-07/guest-scripts.md`, `labs/module-07/drinks-list.md`, `labs/module-07/recommendation-sheet.md`, `labs/module-07/recommendation-model-answers-solution.md`.
Simulations: none for Module 7 (outline §7).
Honest limit repeated in 07.1, 07.2 and 07.4: water cannot reproduce beer foam, carbonation or the pressure in a sparkling-wine bottle.
Recipes: no cocktail specs in this module, so no recipe-inbox file.
Safety: `> [!WARNING]` on sparkling-wine corks (07.4), `> [!CAUTION]` on glass handling (07.1, 07.2, 07.4) and corkscrew blade (07.4), optional tasting note (07.1, 07.3, 07.5). No sabrage (opening with a sword) — named only to say it is not taught.

Key research findings (details in `curriculum/research-inbox/module-07.md`):
- Israel's Ministries of Agriculture and Health launched a new official wine-region map in September 2026: 8 regions, 18 sub-regions, region on label optional, 85% rule (Montefiore, Jerusalem Post, 10 Oct 2026; ice.co.il, 6 Sep 2026). No gov.il page found. Lesson 02.8 still teaches the five legacy registered regions — flagged in the report for the main session.
- Wine service steps and temperatures from the Court of Master Sommeliers Europe Service Standards; categories from the WSET Level 2 Wines specification (April 2026 issue).
- Draught pour, one-inch head, beer-clean glass tests and temperatures from the Brewers Association Draught Beer Quality Manual (2017 edition PDF); bottle pour from Cicerone; style temperatures from the American Homebrewers Association table (after Randy Mosher).
