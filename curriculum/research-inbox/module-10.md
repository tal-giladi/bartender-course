## Professional judgment and assumptions (labelled in the lessons)

- 20% target pour cost for cocktails; rounding up to 2 ILS; price bands; 3% tolerance; order of variance checks; upselling rules; menu section sizes and description length.
- Waste allowances: spirits/liqueurs/bottled syrups 3%, sparkling 10%, mixers 5%, fresh juice 10%, herbs 20%, ginger 10%, house syrups 0%.
- Yields and piece counts: 300 ml/kg lemons and limes, 400 ml/kg grapefruit, ~100 basil leaves per 50 g, ~40 mint leaves per bunch, ~330 ginger slices per kg; syrup volumes from lesson 03.4's densities.
- "Course assumption" prices for products not found at Shufersal online (Old Tom gin, Islay malt, Jamaican and Martinique rums, curaçao, coffee liqueur, elderflower liqueur, Prosecco, orgeat, Angostura, tomato juice) and garnish costs.
- Bars buy at trade prices that are usually lower than retail.
- All sales, counts and waste data are invented for practice.
