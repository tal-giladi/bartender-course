## Professional-judgment statements (no source)

- Yeast stopping around 14–16% ABV (varies by strain) — 02.1.
- Vodka base-material character (wheat soft, rye spicy, potato round); navy strength ~57%; imported brands dominate Israeli back bars — 02.2.
- Rum age statements not uniformly regulated; cristalino not an official class (industry consensus) — 02.3.
- Most Scotch sold is blended — 02.4.
- Arak with grapefruit juice and with lemonade-mint as common Israeli serves; arak as a daily serve in many Israeli bars — 02.5.
- Opened-bottle shelf lives (vermouth ~1 month, fino days, port weeks, wine 1–few days) — 02.7, 02.9.
- Typical ABV ranges for beer (4–6%), cider (4–8%), wine (11–15%); most Israeli draught is pale lager — 02.8.
- Kosher-only venue policies; bartender's role in checking warnings — 02.9.
