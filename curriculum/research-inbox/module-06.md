## Professional judgment (labelled in the lessons, not verified)

- 60 : 22.5 : 22.5 as a starting sour ratio (equals IBA Gin Basil Smash) — 06.1.
- Egg white ≈ 30 ml; Peru often uses lime in the Pisco Sour; pisco has no faithful substitute — 06.2.
- Guests expect cognac in a "Sidecar" / Champagne in a "French 75" (tell them on substitution); built vs shaken Collins speed — 06.2, 06.3, 06.6.
- Rolling by pouring between tins; shaken tomato juice froths and separates; house heat scale 1/3/5 dashes — 06.4.
- Cuba Libre early 20th century and "rum and cola" without lime; Caipirinha traditional Brazilian; 4 tsp sugar ≈ 16 g; elderflower cordial ≈ 20 ml for 40 ml liqueur; honey syrup 2:1 or 3:1 — 06.5.
- Lemon/lime 1:1 swap; alcohol-free drinks need slightly less sugar or more body; "straight up" ambiguity — 06.6.
- Israeli menus bilingual; arak as long drink with grapefruit or lemonade-mint and as a shot; צ'ייסר = shot; kosher certification affects stock and Shabbat hours; menu prices VAT-inclusive (to be verified in Module 10); ice-before-water film explanation for arak; 30 ml arak pour — 06.7.
