# Glossary inbox — Module 9

- **Acknowledge (a guest)** — a one-second signal (eye contact, nod, "one minute") that you have seen a waiting guest, given even while busy (lessons/module-09/lesson-01.md)
- **Modifier** — any change to a standard drink: double, no ice, up, less sweet, a different spirit, alcohol-free (lessons/module-09/lesson-01.md)
- **Tab** — an open bill that collects a guest's orders until they pay (lessons/module-09/lesson-01.md)
- **LAST (listen, apologise, solve, thank)** — a four-step way to handle complaints used in hospitality (lessons/module-09/lesson-02.md)
- **Remake** — making a drink again from scratch in a clean glass with fresh ice, never by topping up the returned one (lessons/module-09/lesson-02.md)
- **Comp** — a drink or item given free; a manager's decision (lessons/module-09/lesson-02.md)
- **Food allergy** — an immune reaction to a food protein; even a trace can cause a reaction (lessons/module-09/lesson-03.md)
- **Intolerance** — a non-immune reaction to a food, usually dose-related (for example lactose intolerance) (lessons/module-09/lesson-03.md)
- **Anaphylaxis** — a severe, life-threatening allergic reaction that usually starts within minutes; call 101 (lessons/module-09/lesson-03.md)
- **The 14 allergens** — the EU Annex II list adopted in Israel: cereals with gluten, crustaceans, eggs, fish, peanuts, soybeans, milk, tree nuts, celery, mustard, sesame, sulphites, lupin, molluscs (lessons/module-09/lesson-03.md)
- **Cross-contact** — an allergen getting into a drink that does not contain it, through tools, ice, hands or garnish (lessons/module-09/lesson-03.md)
- **Clean set** — washed or fresh tools, fresh ice, a garnish from a closed container and washed hands, used for an allergy order (lessons/module-09/lesson-03.md)
- **"May contain"** — a producer's warning on a label that an allergen may be present through cross-contact (lessons/module-09/lesson-03.md)
- **Standard drink** — a fixed amount of pure alcohol used to compare drinks; 14 g in the U.S. definition used on gov.il; no official Israeli definition found (lessons/module-09/lesson-04.md)
- **Intoxication** — a state in which a person's speech, balance, coordination or behaviour is noticeably affected by alcohol (lessons/module-09/lesson-04.md)
- **Refusal of service** — declining to serve alcohol to a guest, done privately and calmly, with an alternative offered (lessons/module-09/lesson-04.md)
- **Alcohol poisoning** — a dangerous level of alcohol in the body; signs include confusion, vomiting, slow breathing and passing out; call 101 (lessons/module-09/lesson-04.md)
- **Incident log** — a written, factual record of refusals, injuries, allergy incidents and emergencies (lessons/module-09/lesson-04.md)
- **Minor** — in Israeli law, a person under 18 (lessons/module-09/lesson-05.md)
- **Presumption of knowledge (s. 193A(c1))** — the rule that a seller who served a minor is presumed to have known, unless the minor showed a document saying they were adult (lessons/module-09/lesson-05.md)
- **Cut-off date** — the latest date of birth that makes a guest 18 today (today minus 18 years) (lessons/module-09/lesson-05.md)
- **Statutory requirement** — a rule in a law or regulation (lessons/module-09/lesson-05.md)
- **Licence condition** — a condition in a business licence, binding on the licensed business, published in the uniform specification for its licensing item (lessons/module-09/lesson-05.md)
- **Uniform specification** — the published set of licence conditions for a type of business under the Licensing of Businesses Law (lessons/module-09/lesson-05.md)
- **Intoxicating drink** — in the Licensing of Businesses Law, a fermented or alcoholic drink with 2% ABV or more (lessons/module-09/lesson-05.md)
- **Holder (of a place)** — the person in charge of a public place or workplace, who carries legal duties (smoking law, safety training) (lessons/module-09/lesson-05.md)
- **Cross-contamination** — transfer of bacteria, allergens or chemicals into food or drink (lessons/module-09/lesson-06.md)
- **HACCP** — Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points, a system for finding and controlling food-safety hazards (lessons/module-09/lesson-06.md)
- **Training log** — the employer's record of safety training given to each worker (lessons/module-09/lesson-06.md)
- **Near miss** — an event that nearly caused harm; reported and logged like an injury (lessons/module-09/lesson-06.md)
- **Safety-critical choice** — in the guest-scenarios simulation and the final assessment, a choice that fails the scenario regardless of points (lessons/module-09/lesson-07.md)

## Hebrew

Usage is professional judgment (common Israeli bar usage as of 2026-10-18) except legal terms, which are the words used in the laws cited.

- Good evening — ערב טוב (erev tov)
- What can I bring you? — מה אפשר להביא לך / לכם? (ma efshar lehavi lecha / lach / lachem) — to a man / woman / group
- The bill — החשבון (hacheshbon)
- Tip — טיפ (tip)
- Sorry, I'm sorry — סליחה, אני מצטער / מצטערת (slicha, ani mitzta'er / mitzta'eret) — said by a man / woman
- Allergy — אלרגיה (alergia)
- Sensitivity, intolerance — רגישות (regishut)
- Tree nuts — אגוזים (egozim)
- Peanuts — בוטנים (botnim)
- Almonds — שקדים (shkedim)
- Sesame — שומשום (shumshum)
- Sulphites — סולפיטים (sulfitim)
- Fava beans — פול (ful) — special labelling rule in Israel
- Contains — מכיל (mechil) — on labels
- May contain — עלול להכיל (alul lehachil) — on labels
- Minor — קטין (katin) — legal term, under 18
- ID document — תעודה מזהה (te'uda mezaha); identity card — תעודת זהות (te'udat zehut)
- Can I see ID, please? — אפשר לראות תעודה מזהה, בבקשה? (efshar lir'ot te'uda mezaha, bevakasha?)
- Intoxicating drink — משקה משכר (mashke meshaker) — legal term
- Drunk — שיכור (shikor) — legal term in Penal Law s. 193
- I can't serve you more alcohol tonight — אני לא יכול / יכולה להגיש לך עוד אלכוהול הערב (ani lo yachol / yechola lehagish lecha od alkohol ha'erev)
- Bar (in law) — בית משקה (beit mashke) — smoking-law schedule
- Uniform specification — מפרט אחיד (mifrat achid) — licence conditions
- Training log — פנקס הדרכה (pinkas hadracha)
