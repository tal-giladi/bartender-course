# Glossary inbox — Module 4

- **Build** — make a drink directly in its serving glass, usually over ice, with the carbonated part added last (lessons/module-04/lesson-01.md)
- **Aeration** — beating tiny air bubbles into a drink, which makes it lighter, frothier and cloudier; strongest when shaking (lessons/module-04/lesson-01.md)
- **Up** — served without ice in a chilled stemmed glass (lessons/module-04/lesson-01.md)
- **On the rocks** — served over ice (lessons/module-04/lesson-01.md)
- **Highball** — a spirit lengthened with a larger amount of carbonated mixer, built over ice in a tall glass; also the name of the glass (lessons/module-04/lesson-02.md)
- **Mixer** — a non-alcoholic ingredient that lengthens a drink, such as soda, tonic, cola or juice (lessons/module-04/lesson-02.md)
- **Top (top up)** — fill the glass to its working level with the mixer (lessons/module-04/lesson-02.md)
- **Lift** — one stroke of the bar spoon from the bottom of a built drink upwards, to mix without losing fizz (lessons/module-04/lesson-02.md)
- **Spritz** — a wine-based built drink with sparkling wine and soda over ice (lessons/module-04/lesson-02.md)
- **Stir** — mix a drink with ice in a mixing glass using a bar spoon, to chill and dilute it without adding air (lessons/module-04/lesson-03.md)
- **Stir count** — the number of spoon turns that matches your standard stir time, used instead of a stopwatch (lessons/module-04/lesson-03.md)
- **Shake** — mix a drink hard with ice in a sealed shaker for 10–15 seconds, to chill, dilute, mix and aerate it (lessons/module-04/lesson-04.md)
- **Boston shaker** — a two-piece shaker of a large tin and a smaller tin (or glass) that seals inside it (lessons/module-04/lesson-04.md)
- **Dry shake** — shaking without ice first, then with ice, to build a thicker foam from egg white or aquafaba (lessons/module-04/lesson-04.md)
- **Reverse dry shake** — shaking with ice first, straining the drink back and shaking again without ice, for the thickest foam (lessons/module-04/lesson-04.md)
- **Whip shake** — a short shake with a little crushed ice, for drinks poured over crushed ice (lessons/module-04/lesson-04.md)
- **Aquafaba** — the liquid from a can of chickpeas, used as a vegan foaming agent instead of egg white (lessons/module-04/lesson-04.md)
- **Pasteurised egg white** — egg white heated enough to kill bacteria such as Salmonella, sold in cartons; safe to use raw (lessons/module-04/lesson-04.md)
- **Double straining (fine straining)** — straining through a Hawthorne and a fine mesh strainer at the same time, to catch ice shards, pulp, herbs and egg (lessons/module-04/lesson-05.md)
- **Muddle** — press fruit, herbs or sugar with a muddler, with a twist, to release juice, oils and flavour (lessons/module-04/lesson-05.md)
- **Layering** — pouring liquids of different densities so they sit in separate bands; heaviest (most sugar, least alcohol) first (lessons/module-04/lesson-05.md)
- **Float** — the last, lightest layer poured on top of a drink (lessons/module-04/lesson-05.md)
- **Blend** — mix a drink with crushed ice in an electric blender to make a frozen texture (lessons/module-04/lesson-06.md)
- **Swizzle** — mix a drink in its glass of crushed ice by spinning a swizzle stick between the palms until the glass frosts (lessons/module-04/lesson-06.md)
- **Churn** — mix a crushed-ice drink by moving a spoon up and down without the palm spin (lessons/module-04/lesson-06.md)
- **Throw** — pour a drink from a high vessel to a low one through a strainer and back, several times, for fine aeration and chill (lessons/module-04/lesson-06.md)
- **Garnish** — anything added to the finished drink for aroma, flavour, look or identification (lessons/module-04/lesson-07.md)
- **Twist (zest)** — a strip of citrus peel, usually expressed over the drink (lessons/module-04/lesson-07.md)
- **Express** — squeeze or twist a citrus peel so its oils spray onto the drink's surface (lessons/module-04/lesson-07.md)
- **Half rim** — salt or sugar on the outside of half the glass rim (lessons/module-04/lesson-07.md)
- **Spread** — the highest minus the lowest result across reps of a drill; a measure of consistency (lessons/module-04/lesson-08.md)
- **Error %** — (measured − target) ÷ target × 100; the course tolerance for measured pours is ±5% (lessons/module-04/lesson-08.md)

## Hebrew

Usage is professional judgment (common Israeli bar usage as of 2026-10-17), not official terms. Dilution, shaking and stirring were introduced in Module 3 (03.5); they are repeated here only for completeness of Module 4's terms.

- Dilution — דילול (dilul) — water added by melting ice
- Shaking — ניעור (ni'ur) — shaking a drink with ice
- Stirring — ערבוב (irbuv) — stirring a drink with ice
- Soda water — סודה (soda) — carbonated water
- Tonic — טוניק (tonik) — tonic water
- Strainer — מסננת (mesanenet) — any bar strainer
- Foam — קצף (ketzef) — foam on top of a drink
- Garnish — קישוט (kishut) — garnish, decoration
- Peel / twist — קליפה (klipa) — citrus peel used as a twist
