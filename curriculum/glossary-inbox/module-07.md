# Glossary inbox — Module 7

- **Head (beer)** — the collar of foam on a poured beer, ideally about 2–3 cm; it carries aroma and is partly beer (lessons/module-07/lesson-01.md)
- **Beer-clean glass** — a glass free of grease, detergent and rinse-aid residues that break beer foam; checked with the sheeting, salt and lacing tests (lessons/module-07/lesson-01.md)
- **Sheeting test** — dipping a glass in water: an even film means beer-clean, droplets mean a film on the glass (lessons/module-07/lesson-01.md)
- **Lacing** — rings of foam left on the inside of a clean glass as a beer is drunk (lessons/module-07/lesson-01.md)
- **Nonic** — a pint glass with a bulge near the top (lessons/module-07/lesson-01.md)
- **Tulip (beer glass)** — a stemmed glass with a round bowl and flared lip, used for strong, Belgian-style and aromatic beers (lessons/module-07/lesson-01.md)
- **Snifter** — a small stemmed glass with a wide bowl and narrow top, used for very strong beers and brandy (lessons/module-07/lesson-01.md)
- **Draught (draft) beer** — beer served from a keg through a tap (lessons/module-07/lesson-02.md)
- **Keg** — a pressurised metal container of draught beer (lessons/module-07/lesson-02.md)
- **Balance (draught system)** — the setting of gas pressure, temperature and line length so beer pours with the right carbonation (lessons/module-07/lesson-02.md)
- **Bottle-conditioned** — beer that has had a second fermentation in the bottle and has yeast at the bottom (lessons/module-07/lesson-02.md)
- **Hefeweizen** — German-style unfiltered wheat beer, served with its yeast (lessons/module-07/lesson-02.md)
- **Tannin** — the drying, gripping feel from grape skins and seeds, mainly in red wine (lessons/module-07/lesson-03.md)
- **Body** — how heavy a wine or drink feels in the mouth; rises with alcohol and sugar (lessons/module-07/lesson-03.md)
- **Vintage** — the harvest year of the grapes in a wine (lessons/module-07/lesson-03.md)
- **Varietal wine** — a wine named after its main grape variety (lessons/module-07/lesson-03.md)
- **Kosher wine** — wine made under kosher supervision, handled only by Jews from crushing onwards (lessons/module-07/lesson-03.md)
- **Mevushal** — kosher wine that has been heat-treated, so it stays kosher when anyone opens and pours it (lessons/module-07/lesson-03.md)
- **Wine region (Israel, 2026 map)** — one of eight official regions with 18 sub-regions; a label naming one needs at least 85% of the grapes from it (lessons/module-07/lesson-03.md)
- **Waiter's friend** — a folding corkscrew with a foil blade, a worm and a lever (lessons/module-07/lesson-04.md)
- **Worm** — the spiral of a corkscrew (lessons/module-07/lesson-04.md)
- **Cage (muselet)** — the wire cage holding a sparkling-wine cork (lessons/module-07/lesson-04.md)
- **Cork taint ("corked")** — a fault that gives wine a musty, wet-cardboard smell (lessons/module-07/lesson-04.md)
- **Heat damage** — a fault from storing wine too warm: cooked, jammy taste (lessons/module-07/lesson-04.md)
- **Alcohol-free (UK)** — alcohol removed to no more than 0.05% ABV (lessons/module-07/lesson-05.md)
- **De-alcoholised** — alcohol removed to no more than 0.5% ABV (UK guidance) (lessons/module-07/lesson-05.md)
- **Low alcohol (UK)** — 1.2% ABV or less (lessons/module-07/lesson-05.md)
- **Worty** — a sweet, grainy taste of unfermented beer, common in some alcohol-free beers (lessons/module-07/lesson-05.md)
- **Zero-proof drink** — a mixed drink with no alcohol, also called a mocktail (lessons/module-07/lesson-05.md)
- **Upselling** — suggesting a better or additional drink when it fits the guest's wishes (lessons/module-07/lesson-06.md)

## Hebrew

Usage is professional judgment (common Israeli bar usage as of 2026-10-17) except the wine-region names, which are from the 2026 map as reported by ice.co.il, and kashrut terms.

- Foam / head — קצף (ketzef) — the head on a beer
- Tap — ברז (berez) — the draught faucet
- Keg — חבית (chavit) — also "barrel"
- Draught beer — בירה מהחבית (bira mehachavit)
- Bottle — בקבוק (bakbuk)
- Can — פחית (pachit)
- Pint — פיינט (paint) — a pint glass or a pint of beer
- Third (of a litre) — שליש (shlish) — a 330 ml beer
- Half (litre) — חצי (chatzi) — a 500 ml beer
- Change the keg — להחליף חבית (lehachlif chavit)
- Open the tap all the way — פתח את הברז עד הסוף (ptach et haberez ad hasof)
- Wine glass — כוס יין (kos yayin)
- Corkscrew (waiter's friend) — פותחן מלצרים (potchan meltzarim)
- Cork — פקק (pkak) — also any bottle cap
- Kashrut — כשרות (kashrut) — kosher certification
- Mevushal — מבושל (mevushal) — "cooked"; heat-treated kosher wine
- Golan — הגולן (haGolan) — wine region
- Galilee — הגליל (haGalil) — wine region
- Valleys — העמקים (ha'Amakim) — wine region
- Carmel — הכרמל (haCarmel) — wine region
- Foothills — מורדות ההר (mordot hahar) — wine region
- Coastal Plain — מישור החוף (mishor hachof) — wine region
- Central Mountains — ההר המרכזי (hahar hamerkazi) — wine region
- Negev — הנגב (haNegev) — wine region
- Alcohol-free — נטול אלכוהול (natul alkohol)
- Without alcohol — ללא אלכוהול (lelo alkohol)
- Zero (as in "zero beer") — זירו (ziro)
- "Black beer" — בירה שחורה (bira shchora) — usually a sweet malt soft drink, not a dark beer; read the label
