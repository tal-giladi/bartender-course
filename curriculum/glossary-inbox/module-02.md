# Glossary inbox — Module 2

- **ABV (alcohol by volume)** — the percentage of a drink's volume that is pure ethanol, measured at 20 °C; written "% vol" on labels (lessons/module-02/lesson-01.md)
- **Ageing (maturation)** — resting a spirit or wine in wooden barrels so it gains colour, softness and aromas such as vanilla and spice (lessons/module-02/lesson-01.md)
- **Distillation** — heating a fermented liquid so the alcohol evaporates before water and is collected as a stronger spirit (lessons/module-02/lesson-01.md)
- **Fermentation** — yeast turning sugar into ethanol and carbon dioxide (lessons/module-02/lesson-01.md)
- **Heads, heart, tails** — the first, middle and last parts of a distillation run; only the heart is kept for drinking (lessons/module-02/lesson-01.md)
- **Pot still / column still** — batch still that keeps more flavour / continuous tower still that reaches higher strength (lessons/module-02/lesson-01.md)
- **Proof (US)** — twice the ABV; 80 proof = 40% ABV (lessons/module-02/lesson-01.md)
- **Standard drink** — a fixed amount of pure alcohol used in health advice; about 14 g in the Israeli Ministry of Health sheet (lessons/module-02/lesson-01.md)
- **Botanicals** — plant flavourings in gin, such as juniper, coriander seed, citrus peel and angelica (lessons/module-02/lesson-02.md)
- **London dry gin** — gin re-distilled with natural botanicals, with nothing added afterwards except water and a trace of sugar; a method, not a place (lessons/module-02/lesson-02.md)
- **Navy strength** — gin bottled at about 57% ABV (lessons/module-02/lesson-02.md)
- **Vodka** — neutral spirit, at least 37.5% ABV in the EU and 40% in the US (lessons/module-02/lesson-02.md)
- **100% agave** — tequila whose sugars all come from blue Weber agave; without it, tequila may contain up to 49% other sugars (lessons/module-02/lesson-03.md)
- **Blanco, reposado, añejo, extra añejo** — tequila age classes: unaged, at least 2 months, at least 1 year, at least 3 years in oak (lessons/module-02/lesson-03.md)
- **Mezcal** — Mexican agave spirit from many agave species, often smoky; categories mezcal, artesanal and ancestral (lessons/module-02/lesson-03.md)
- **Rhum agricole** — rum made from fresh sugar-cane juice, mostly in the French Caribbean (lessons/module-02/lesson-03.md)
- **Bourbon** — US whiskey from at least 51% corn, aged in new charred oak, distilled to no more than 80% ABV (lessons/module-02/lesson-04.md)
- **Cognac** — grape brandy from the Cognac region of France; VS, VSOP, Napoléon and XO mean at least 2, 4, 6 and 10 years (lessons/module-02/lesson-04.md)
- **Peat** — decomposed plant matter burned to dry malted barley, giving smoky whisky (lessons/module-02/lesson-04.md)
- **Scotch whisky** — whisky made and matured in Scotland for at least three years in oak (lessons/module-02/lesson-04.md)
- **Arak** — dry, usually grape-based anise spirit of the Levant, often 50% ABV or more (lessons/module-02/lesson-05.md)
- **Louche** — the milky cloud that forms when water is added to an anise spirit (lessons/module-02/lesson-05.md)
- **Pisco** — grape brandy from Peru and Chile (lessons/module-02/lesson-05.md)
- **Cachaça** — Brazilian spirit distilled from fresh sugar-cane juice (lessons/module-02/lesson-05.md)
- **Bitters** — concentrated, unsweetened infusions of roots, barks and spices in strong alcohol, used by the dash (lessons/module-02/lesson-06.md)
- **Dash** — a quick flick from a bitters bottle, about 0.8 ml in this course (lessons/module-02/lesson-06.md)
- **Liqueur** — sweetened, flavoured spirit; in the EU at least 15% ABV and 100 g sugar per litre for most types (lessons/module-02/lesson-06.md)
- **Aperitif** — a drink served before a meal to open the appetite (lessons/module-02/lesson-07.md)
- **Aromatised wine** — wine flavoured with herbs and spices, with added alcohol; vermouth is one (lessons/module-02/lesson-07.md)
- **Flor** — the layer of yeast that protects fino and manzanilla sherry from air (lessons/module-02/lesson-07.md)
- **Fortified wine** — wine with added grape spirit, such as sherry and port (lessons/module-02/lesson-07.md)
- **Oxidation** — the slow change caused by air after opening; makes vermouth and wine flat or sour (lessons/module-02/lesson-07.md)
- **Vermouth** — aromatised wine flavoured with wormwood, 14.5% to under 22% ABV in the EU (lessons/module-02/lesson-07.md)
- **Ale / lager** — beer fermented with top-fermenting yeast at warmer temperatures / bottom-fermenting yeast at cool temperatures (lessons/module-02/lesson-08.md)
- **Brut** — sparkling wine label term; drier than "extra dry" (lessons/module-02/lesson-08.md)
- **Traditional method / tank method** — second fermentation of sparkling wine in the bottle / in a pressure tank (lessons/module-02/lesson-08.md)
- **Strong alcoholic beverage (Israeli law)** — a drink above 15.5% ABV, which must carry the stronger statutory warning (lessons/module-02/lesson-09.md)
