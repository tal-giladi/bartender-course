# Glossary inbox — Module 05

- **Spec** — the exact written version of a drink: ingredients with amounts, method, glass and ice, garnish (lessons/module-05/lesson-01.md)
- **House spec** — a bar's own written version of a drink, used by every bartender there (lessons/module-05/lesson-01.md)
- **IBA** — International Bartenders Association; publishes the official list of classic cocktail specs in ml (lessons/module-05/lesson-01.md)
- **Base** — the main spirit of a drink, usually the largest amount (lessons/module-05/lesson-01.md)
- **Modifier** — a smaller ingredient that changes the base's flavour, such as vermouth or a liqueur (lessons/module-05/lesson-01.md)
- **Lengthener** — a long mixer, usually fizzy, that tops up a drink (soda, ginger beer, Prosecco) (lessons/module-05/lesson-01.md)
- **Active recall (testing effect)** — remembering from memory and then checking, which fixes memory better than rereading (lessons/module-05/lesson-01.md)
- **Spaced repetition** — reviewing at growing intervals, with hard items more often (lessons/module-05/lesson-01.md)
- **Leitner system** — flashcards in numbered boxes; a correct card moves up a box, a missed card returns to box 1 (lessons/module-05/lesson-01.md)
- **Interleaving** — mixing different kinds of items in one practice session (lessons/module-05/lesson-01.md)
- **Up** — served without ice in a stemmed glass chilled beforehand (lessons/module-05/lesson-02.md)
- **Dry / wet (Martini)** — less / more vermouth in proportion to gin (lessons/module-05/lesson-02.md)
- **Twist** — a strip of citrus peel whose oil is expressed over the drink (lessons/module-05/lesson-02.md)
- **Perfect (Manhattan)** — vermouth split between sweet and dry (lessons/module-05/lesson-02.md)
- **Few dashes** — an unfixed small number of dashes, usually 2–3; follow the house spec (lessons/module-05/lesson-03.md)
- **Sbagliato** — a Negroni with Prosecco instead of gin (lessons/module-05/lesson-03.md)
- **Sour** — base spirit + citrus + sweetener, shaken (lessons/module-05/lesson-04.md)
- **Triple sec** — a clear orange liqueur; Cointreau is one brand (lessons/module-05/lesson-04.md)
- **Superfine (caster) sugar** — very fine white sugar that dissolves quickly (lessons/module-05/lesson-04.md)
- **Half salt rim** — salt on the outside of half the glass rim, so the guest can choose (lessons/module-05/lesson-04.md)
- **100% agave tequila** — tequila made only from agave sugars (lessons/module-05/lesson-04.md)
- **Aquafaba** — the liquid from cooked or canned chickpeas, used as an egg-free foamer (lessons/module-05/lesson-05.md)
- **Pasteurised egg white** — egg white heat-treated to kill Salmonella; safe to use raw in drinks (lessons/module-05/lesson-05.md)
- **Dry shake** — shaking without ice first to build foam, then with ice (lessons/module-05/lesson-05.md)
- **Reverse dry shake** — shaking with ice first, then again without ice for thicker foam (lessons/module-05/lesson-05.md)
- **Citrus vodka** — vodka flavoured with lemon or other citrus (lessons/module-05/lesson-05.md)
- **Cobbler glass** — a small goblet named in the IBA Whiskey Sour spec (lessons/module-05/lesson-05.md)
- **Mint sprig** — a small stem of mint with its leaves (lessons/module-05/lesson-06.md)
- **Ginger beer** — a fizzy, spicy ginger soft drink, hotter and less sweet than ginger ale (lessons/module-05/lesson-06.md)
- **Lined copper mug** — a copper mug with a stainless steel or nickel lining, safe for acidic drinks (lessons/module-05/lesson-06.md)
- **Spritz** — a bitter aperitif lengthened with sparkling wine and soda over ice (lessons/module-05/lesson-07.md)
- **3-2-1** — Aperol's ratio: 3 parts Prosecco, 2 Aperol, 1 soda (90/60/30 ml) (lessons/module-05/lesson-07.md)
- **Crema** — the fine foam on fresh espresso, made of coffee oils and gas (lessons/module-05/lesson-07.md)
- **Mise en place** — preparing everything before service starts (lessons/module-05/lesson-08.md)

## Hebrew

Usage is professional judgment (common Israeli bar usage as of 2026-10-17), not official terms.

- Recipe / spec — מתכון (matkon) — recipe; also used for a bar's spec
- Cocktail — קוקטייל (kokteyl) — cocktail
- Garnish — קישוט (kishut) — decoration on the drink
- Twist — טוויסט (tvist) — citrus peel twist, borrowed from English
- Lemon peel — קליפת לימון (klipat limon)
- Orange slice — פלח תפוז (pelach tapuz) — also used for a wheel or half wheel
- Lime — ליים (laym) — lime; לימון ירוק (limon yarok) is also heard
- Mint — נענע (nana)
- Sugar cube — קוביית סוכר (kubiyat sukar)
- Egg white — חלבון (chelbon)
- Foam — קצף (ketsef)
- Chickpeas — חומוס (chumus) — source of aquafaba
- Salt rim — שפת מלח (sfat melach) — salted rim of the glass
- Ginger beer — ג'ינג'ר ביר (jinjer bir)
- Espresso — אספרסו (espreso)
- Coffee beans — פולי קפה (polei kafe)
- Muddler — מדוך (maduch) — muddler, from "to crush"
- On the rocks — על קרח (al kerach) — over ice
- Straight up — בלי קרח (bli kerach) — without ice
