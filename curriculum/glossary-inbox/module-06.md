# Glossary inbox — Module 6

- **Cocktail family** — a group of drinks that share one structure: the same kinds of ingredients in similar proportions, the same method and usually the same glass (lessons/module-06/lesson-01.md)
- **Lengthener** — a large, usually non-alcoholic mixer that makes a drink long, such as soda, tonic, ginger beer or sparkling wine (lessons/module-06/lesson-01.md)
- **Aromatised wine** — wine flavoured with herbs and spices and slightly fortified, such as vermouth (lessons/module-06/lesson-01.md)
- **Collins** — a sour lengthened with soda water and served in a tall glass full of ice (lessons/module-06/lesson-01.md)
- **Fizz** — a shaken sour topped with a splash of soda and served in a tall glass without ice (lessons/module-06/lesson-01.md)
- **Tiki** — a style of rum drink from mid-20th-century American "Polynesian" restaurants: several rums, lime, syrups, crushed ice and big garnishes (lessons/module-06/lesson-01.md)
- **Triple sec** — a clear, sweet orange liqueur used as the sweetener in sours such as the White Lady, Sidecar and Margarita (lessons/module-06/lesson-02.md)
- **Pisco** — an unaged grape brandy from Peru and Chile (lessons/module-06/lesson-02.md)
- **Amargo bitters** — Peruvian aromatic bitters dropped on the foam of a Pisco Sour; Angostura is the usual substitute (lessons/module-06/lesson-02.md)
- **Old Tom gin** — an older, slightly sweeter and softer style of gin; the IBA uses it for the Tom Collins (lessons/module-06/lesson-03.md)
- **John Collins** — the IBA's Collins spec (gin, lemon, syrup, soda, built on ice); with Old Tom gin it becomes a Tom Collins (lessons/module-06/lesson-03.md)
- **Brut** — dry, as a style of sparkling wine (lessons/module-06/lesson-03.md)
- **Savory drink** — a drink whose main character comes from salt, spice, tomato or other non-sweet flavours, such as the Bloody Mary (lessons/module-06/lesson-04.md)
- **Roll (rolling)** — mixing a drink with ice by tipping the closed shaker over and over, or pouring it between two tins, to chill it without frothing (lessons/module-06/lesson-04.md)
- **Orgeat** — a sweet almond syrup used in the Mai Tai; contains tree nuts (lessons/module-06/lesson-04.md)
- **Orange curaçao** — an orange liqueur, close to triple sec, used in the Mai Tai (lessons/module-06/lesson-04.md)
- **Lime cordial** — sweetened, preserved lime juice concentrate; the classic Gimlet ingredient (lessons/module-06/lesson-05.md)
- **Cachaça** — Brazilian spirit distilled from fresh sugar-cane juice, the base of the Caipirinha (lessons/module-06/lesson-05.md)
- **Amaro** — a bittersweet Italian herbal liqueur, such as Amaro Nonino in the Paper Plane (lessons/module-06/lesson-05.md)
- **Float** — a small amount of liquid poured gently on top of a drink so it stays as a layer for the first sips (lessons/module-06/lesson-05.md)
- **Elderflower liqueur** — a sweet liqueur flavoured with elder blossoms, used in the Hugo Spritz (lessons/module-06/lesson-05.md)
- **Caipiroska / Caipirissima** — a Caipirinha made with vodka / with rum (IBA note) (lessons/module-06/lesson-06.md)
- **House spec** — a bar's own written version of a drink, which may differ from the IBA spec (lessons/module-06/lesson-06.md)
- **Straight up** — ambiguous order: some guests mean "up" (chilled and strained, no ice), others "neat" (room temperature, no ice); ask (lessons/module-06/lesson-06.md)
- **Professional judgment** — a statement based on experience, not on a published source; labelled as such in this course (lessons/module-06/lesson-07.md)
- **Cross-utilisation** — choosing menu drinks that share ingredients, to keep prep short and waste low (lessons/module-06/lesson-08.md)

## Hebrew

Usage is professional judgment (common Israeli bar usage as of 2026-10-17), not official terms. Limonana's etymology is from Wikipedia (lesson 06.7).

- Menu — תפריט (tafrit) — the list of drinks and food
- Cocktail — קוקטייל (kokteyl) — a mixed drink
- Shot — צ'ייסר (chaser) — in Israeli bars, a small glass of spirit drunk in one go
- Grapefruit — אשכולית (eshkolit) — the fruit and its juice, as in arak with grapefruit
- Arak with grapefruit — ערק אשכוליות (arak eshkoliyot) — the long arak serve
- Mint lemonade — לימונענע (limonana) — lemonade blended or served with mint
- Tomato juice — מיץ עגבניות (mitz agvaniyot) — base of the Bloody Mary
- Egg white — חלבון (chelbon) — foaming agent in sours
- Almonds — שקדים (shkedim) — the nut in orgeat; allergen
- Substitute — תחליף (tachlif) — an ingredient used in place of another
- Alcohol-free — נטול אלכוהול (netul alkohol) — without alcohol
- Sparkling wine — יין מבעבע (yayin meva'bea) — wine with bubbles, such as Prosecco or cava
