# Glossary inbox — Module 8

- **Opening (bar opening)** — the routine that takes the bar from locked and empty to ready for the first guest: checks, temperatures, stock pull, prep, setup and briefing (lessons/module-08/lesson-01.md)
- **Close (bar close)** — the routine from last orders to locked door: tabs closed, prep stored or discarded, cleaning, restocking, logs and handover note (lessons/module-08/lesson-01.md)
- **Par (par level)** — the amount of an item the bar wants on hand after each delivery or at the start of a shift (lessons/module-08/lesson-01.md)
- **Temperature log** — a written record of fridge and freezer readings with date, time, initials and any action taken (lessons/module-08/lesson-01.md)
- **Handover note** — a short written note for the next shift: what ran out, what was ordered, what broke, what prep is in the fridge (lessons/module-08/lesson-01.md)
- **Briefing** — a few minutes before opening when the team hears bookings, specials, the 86 list, allergen news and roles (lessons/module-08/lesson-01.md)
- **Mise en place** — French for "put in place": all prep, garnish, equipment and stock ready before service (lessons/module-08/lesson-02.md)
- **Prep list** — a list of what to prepare and how much, calculated from the forecast plus a buffer minus what is already in date (lessons/module-08/lesson-02.md)
- **Forecast** — the expected number of each drink for a shift, usually based on past sales (lessons/module-08/lesson-02.md)
- **Buffer** — extra prep on top of the forecast for spills, remakes and a busier night; 10% in this course as a starting point (lessons/module-08/lesson-02.md)
- **Date marking** — labelling prepared food with the date (and time) it was made or must be discarded (lessons/module-08/lesson-02.md)
- **Pre-mix** — a batch of a drink's ingredients without water, still shaken or stirred on ice for each serving (lessons/module-08/lesson-03.md)
- **Pre-diluted batch** — a batch that already contains the water ice would have added, poured straight into a chilled glass (lessons/module-08/lesson-03.md)
- **Planned dilution** — the water added to a pre-diluted batch, as a fraction of the undiluted batch volume (lessons/module-08/lesson-03.md)
- **Headspace** — empty space left at the top of a bottle so the contents can expand (lessons/module-08/lesson-03.md)
- **Inventory** — all the stock a bar holds for sale (lessons/module-08/lesson-04.md)
- **Stock count** — recording how much of each item is on hand at a set time (lessons/module-08/lesson-04.md)
- **Usage** — opening count plus received minus closing count (lessons/module-08/lesson-04.md)
- **Variance** — usage minus what the POS says was sold; variance % is variance divided by sold (lessons/module-08/lesson-04.md)
- **Tolerance** — the variance a bar accepts before investigating; set by the bar, not by law (lessons/module-08/lesson-04.md)
- **Waste log** — a record of every ml lost without a sale: spills, breakage, remakes, expired prep, with reason and initials (lessons/module-08/lesson-04.md)
- **Safety stock** — extra stock above expected usage to cover a busy week or a late delivery (lessons/module-08/lesson-04.md)
- **Order quantity** — par minus on hand, rounded up to whole units (lessons/module-08/lesson-04.md)
- **86 (eighty-six)** — bar and restaurant slang for an item that is finished for the night (also for refusing service to a guest) (lessons/module-08/lesson-07.md)
- **Ticket** — one order sent to the bar from the POS, a server or bar guests (lessons/module-08/lesson-05.md)
- **Ticket time** — the time from a ticket's arrival until all its drinks are served (lessons/module-08/lesson-05.md)
- **Round** — several drinks for one group, often identical, made together (lessons/module-08/lesson-05.md)
- **Quick win** — a short ticket (about 30 seconds or less) made before an older, longer one, once, to cut average waiting (lessons/module-08/lesson-05.md)
- **Dead drink** — a finished drink that waited too long before serving and is watery, warm or flat; in the service simulation, more than 30 seconds (lessons/module-08/lesson-05.md)
- **Glasswasher** — a machine that washes and sanitises racks of glasses with a very hot final rinse or a chemical sanitiser (lessons/module-08/lesson-06.md)
- **Soda gun (bar gun)** — a hand-held dispenser that mixes carbonated water and syrups from boxes under the bar (lessons/module-08/lesson-06.md)
- **Draught line cleaning** — cleaning beer lines with a caustic cleaner, about every two weeks per the Brewers Association (lessons/module-08/lesson-06.md)
- **Keg dolly** — a small wheeled trolley for moving kegs without lifting them (lessons/module-08/lesson-06.md)
- **Call-out** — a short standard phrase that warns colleagues what you are doing, such as "behind", "corner", "hot", "knife", "glass" (lessons/module-08/lesson-07.md)
- **Check-back (closed-loop communication)** — the receiver repeats a message back and the sender confirms it (lessons/module-08/lesson-07.md)
- **Pass** — the spot where finished drinks wait for servers to collect them (lessons/module-08/lesson-07.md)
- **Modifier** — a change to a standard drink on a ticket, such as "no ice", "double" or "alcohol-free" (lessons/module-08/lesson-07.md)

## Hebrew

Usage is professional judgment (common Israeli bar and kitchen usage as of 2026-10-17), not official terms.

- Mise en place — מיזנפלס (mizanplas) — the prep, from French; הכנות (hachanot) is also used
- Storeroom — מחסן (machsan) — where backup stock is kept
- Fridge — מקרר (mekarer)
- Freezer — מקפיא (makpi)
- Thermometer — מדחום (madchom)
- Briefing — תדריך (tadrich) — pre-shift team briefing
- Handover — חפיפה (chafifa) — passing a shift or station to the next person
- Inventory — מלאי (melai)
- Stock count — ספירת מלאי (sfirat melai)
- Waste / shrinkage — פחת (pachat) — business word for stock lost without a sale
- Supplier — ספק (sapak)
- Delivery — אספקה (aspaka)
- Ticket — בון (bon) — an order ticket, from kitchen usage
- Round of drinks — סיבוב (sivuv)
- Till / POS — קופה (kupa)
- Gas cylinder — בלון גז (balon gaz) — CO2 cylinder for soda and draught
- Behind you — מאחוריך / מאחורייך (me'achorecha / me'achorayich) — call-out, to a man / to a woman
- Corner — פינה (pina) — call-out at a blind corner
- Hot — חם (cham) — call-out when carrying something hot
- Excuse me, coming through — סליחה, עובר / עוברת (slicha, over / overet)
- Heard / got it — קיבלתי (kibalti) — confirming an order or request
