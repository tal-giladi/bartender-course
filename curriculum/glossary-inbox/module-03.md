# Glossary inbox — Module 03

- **Centilitre (cl)** — 10 ml; used on older European specs (lessons/module-03/lesson-01.md)
- **Ratio** — a comparison of amounts, e.g. 2 : 1 (lessons/module-03/lesson-01.md)
- **Parts** — a recipe written as a ratio, so it works at any size; ml per part = total ÷ sum of parts (lessons/module-03/lesson-01.md)
- **Scaling** — multiplying every ingredient by the same number of drinks to keep the balance (lessons/module-03/lesson-01.md)
- **Batch** — a quantity of a drink mixed ahead of time (lessons/module-03/lesson-01.md)
- **Planned dilution** — water added to a batch served without ice-mixing, as a fraction of the undiluted volume (lessons/module-03/lesson-01.md)
- **Accuracy** — how close a pour is to its target (lessons/module-03/lesson-02.md)
- **Consistency** — how close repeated pours are to each other (lessons/module-03/lesson-02.md)
- **Tolerance** — how far a pour may be off and still count as right; ±5% in this course (lessons/module-03/lesson-02.md)
- **Error %** — (measured − target) ÷ target × 100 (lessons/module-03/lesson-02.md)
- **Density** — weight of 1 ml of a liquid; water 1.00, 1:1 syrup 1.23, 2:1 syrup about 1.33 g/ml (lessons/module-03/lesson-02.md)
- **Viscous** — thick and slow-flowing, like syrup; clings to a jigger (lessons/module-03/lesson-02.md)
- **Free pouring** — pouring from a bottle with a speed pourer, timed with a count instead of a jigger (lessons/module-03/lesson-02.md)
- **Speed pourer** — a spout fitted to a bottle that gives a steady stream (lessons/module-03/lesson-02.md)
- **Calibrate** — check a count or tool against a known measure and adjust it (lessons/module-03/lesson-02.md)
- **Basic tastes** — sweet, sour, bitter, salty and umami, detected by the tongue (lessons/module-03/lesson-03.md)
- **Aroma** — what the nose detects; most of what we call flavour (lessons/module-03/lesson-03.md)
- **Retronasal smell** — smelling from inside the mouth while swallowing and breathing out (lessons/module-03/lesson-03.md)
- **Texture (mouthfeel)** — body, astringency, fizz and alcohol warmth felt in the mouth (lessons/module-03/lesson-03.md)
- **Astringency** — the dry, gripping feeling from tannin, e.g. strong tea (lessons/module-03/lesson-03.md)
- **Balance** — no element of a drink sticks out unpleasantly (lessons/module-03/lesson-03.md)
- **Saline** — salt solution (20% by weight in this course) added by the drop to soften bitterness (lessons/module-03/lesson-03.md)
- **Sugar syrup (simple syrup)** — sugar dissolved in water; 1:1 or 2:1 by weight (lessons/module-03/lesson-04.md)
- **Rich syrup** — 2:1 sugar syrup (lessons/module-03/lesson-04.md)
- **Degrees Brix (°Bx)** — grams of sugar per 100 g of solution (lessons/module-03/lesson-04.md)
- **Refractometer** — optical tool that measures sugar content (Brix) (lessons/module-03/lesson-04.md)
- **Yield** — usable juice from a quantity of fruit, per piece or per 100 g (lessons/module-03/lesson-04.md)
- **Untreated juice** — fresh, unpasteurised juice; keep refrigerated (lessons/module-03/lesson-04.md)
- **Topping up** — pouring new prep on top of old; not allowed (lessons/module-03/lesson-04.md)
- **Dilution %** — (weight after − weight before) ÷ weight before × 100 (lessons/module-03/lesson-05.md)
- **Holdback** — liquid left clinging to the ice after straining (lessons/module-03/lesson-05.md)
- **Carbonation** — carbon dioxide dissolved in a liquid under pressure (lessons/module-03/lesson-06.md)
- **Henry's law** — the amount of gas dissolved in a liquid is proportional to that gas's pressure above it (lessons/module-03/lesson-06.md)
- **Nucleation site** — a scratch, ice surface or particle where bubbles form and gas escapes (lessons/module-03/lesson-06.md)
- **Carbonic acid** — weak acid formed by dissolved CO2; makes fizzy drinks taste slightly sour (lessons/module-03/lesson-06.md)
- **Highball** — a spirit topped with a larger amount of mixer, built on ice in a tall glass (lessons/module-03/lesson-06.md)
- **Soda gun** — hand-held dispenser for carbonated water and soft drinks behind a bar (lessons/module-03/lesson-06.md)
- **Rescue** — fixing a drink by adding something small, rather than remaking it (lessons/module-03/lesson-07.md)
- **Under-diluted / over-diluted** — too little or too much water from ice for the drink (lessons/module-03/lesson-07.md)

## Hebrew

Usage is professional judgment (common Israeli bar and kitchen usage as of 2026-10-17), not official terms.

- Ratio — יחס (yachas) — ratio between ingredients
- Batch — באץ' (batch) — drink mixed ahead
- Dilution — דילול (dilul) — water added by ice
- Speed pourer — פורר (porer) — pour spout, borrowed from English
- Taste — טעם (ta'am) — taste
- Flavour — טעם וריח (ta'am ve-re'ach) — taste and smell together
- Sweet — מתוק (matok)
- Sour — חמוץ (chamutz)
- Bitter — מר (mar)
- Salty — מלוח (maluach)
- Umami — אומאמי (umami)
- Sugar syrup — סירופ סוכר (sirop sukar)
- Label — תווית (tavit)
- Shaking — ניעור (ni'ur)
- Stirring — ערבוב (irbuv)
- Carbon dioxide — פחמן דו-חמצני (pachman du-chamtsani) — CO2
- Carbonated — מוגז (mugaz) — fizzy
- Soda water — סודה (soda)
