# Module 04 plan — Mixing Methods and Technical Skills

Planning file, not imported. Written 2026-10-17.

Module practical piece: 04.8 filmed technique drills — 10 reps per movement (measure, build, stir, shake, strain, muddle, garnish, reset), accuracy and time logged in `labs/module-04/technique-drill-log.csv`, compared with written demonstrations (Difford's Guide technique pages), self-scored with `templates/technique-rubric.md` (the same rubric 11.3 uses; minimum 75%).
Simulations linked: dilution-lab `stir` (04.3), `shake` (04.4), `throw` (04.6); all three again in 04.8.
No full cocktail specs are given (Modules 5–6 do that), so no recipe inbox. Named drinks are linked to their IBA pages as method examples only. Practice drinks are water/tea/syrup/citrus/soda drills.

Dilution numbers follow `simulations/common/bar-math.js` `dilutionFraction` (linear educational model anchored on Arnold, as committed with Module 3): shaken sour ≈25% ABV → ≈63% water at 12 s (plateau 66%); stirred spirit-forward ≈32% ABV → ≈37% at 20 s, ≈44% at 30 s, ≈48% at 45 s (plateau 51%); thrown ≈33% ABV → ≈39% at 10 s, ≈35% at about 8 s (Difford's aims 30–35%); built highball ≈10% ABV → ≈3% in 30 s, ≈14% after 5 min. Plain water: shake 12 s ≈19%, stir 20–25 s ≈16–18%, build 30 s ≈2%. Water drills train consistency (rep-to-rep spread); measured water gains run a few points under the model because of holdback on the ice.
