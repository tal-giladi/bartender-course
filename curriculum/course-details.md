# Course details (for registering the course in the Academy)

- Suggested slug: `bartending`
- GitHub repo: `tal-giladi/bartender-course` (public)
- Free or paid: suggestion — paid (job-oriented vocational course with calculators and simulations). Tal decides.
- Risk notice: For adults 18+. Involves alcohol, glass, knives and raw egg; practise mostly with water and alcohol-free ingredients, taste only small amounts, never drink and drive, and follow Israeli law on alcohol sale and service.
- One-line summary: Learn to work behind a bar — tools, spirits, technique, the classic cocktails, speed, costing and responsible service under Israeli law — practising at home.
- Who it is for: adults with no bar experience who want an entry-level bartending job in Israel, or who want to make proper drinks at home.
- Format: self-paced, 11 modules, 80 lessons, ≈ 55–65 hours over 8–10 weeks; home drills with water and inexpensive equipment, alcohol-free experiments, interactive simulations (service rush, guest scenarios, recipe drill, costing calculator), quizzes and a graded final assessment. Not an accredited qualification.
- Prerequisites: none. Basic arithmetic; a phone with a camera; about ₪250–400 of basic equipment (alternatives from a normal kitchen are given).
- What you will learn:
  - Set up and reset a bar station and use every common tool, glass and type of ice correctly.
  - Know the main spirits, liqueurs, wines and beers, read labels and make sensible substitutions.
  - Measure accurately, scale recipes and control dilution, temperature and balance.
  - Build, stir, shake, strain, muddle, layer and garnish cleanly and consistently.
  - Make 30+ classic cocktails from memory and understand the families behind them.
  - Work fast under pressure: sequence orders, batch, manage stock and keep the station clean.
  - Serve responsibly: check age, handle intoxication, allergies and complaints, within Israeli law.
  - Cost drinks, set menu prices and reconcile inventory.
