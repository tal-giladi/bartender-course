## Final assessment at a glance

| Component | Weight | Minimum |
|---|---|---|
| Written knowledge (Module 11 quiz) | 20% | 70% |
| Recipe recall | 25% | 80% |
| Practical technique (filmed, self-scored) | 25% | 75% |
| Simulated service | 20% | 70% |
| Costing and operations | 10% | 70% |

Pass = weighted total of at least 80%, every minimum met and no safety-critical failure. Details are in [lesson 11.1](lessons/module-11/lesson-01.md).
