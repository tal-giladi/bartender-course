# Module 7 labs — Beer, Wine, Sparkling Drinks and Beverage Service

Worksheets, scoresheets and role-play material for the Module 7 hands-on work and the module's practical piece (lesson 07.6, a ten-guest recommendation role-play). Most practice uses water, glasses, a corkscrew and labels; tasting is optional, small (sip and spit, under one standard drink per session), 18+ only and never before driving. Water cannot reproduce beer foam, carbonation or the pressure in a sparkling-wine bottle; each lesson says what its practice cannot teach.

| File | Lesson | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `glass-audit.md` | 07.1 | Sheeting and salt tests on four glasses, glass-to-style matching, fridge temperatures |
| `pour-drill.csv` | 07.2 | 20 timed water pours to a fill line on 330 ml and 500 ml glasses (target ±10 g) |
| `wine-label-analysis.csv` | 07.3 | Six wine labels: grape, region (old or 2026 system), vintage, ABV, kosher, mevushal |
| `grape-guest-cards.md` | 07.3 | Twelve guest requests to match to the eight principal grapes |
| `grape-guest-cards-solution.md` | 07.3 | Best matches, alternatives and reasons — check after answering |
| `wine-service-checklist.md` | 07.4 | Scored checklist for still-wine opening, sparkling hand positions and the chilling race |
| `zero-proof-worksheet.md` | 07.5 | Alcohol-free product comparison and four zero-proof serves |
| `drinks-list.md` | 07.6 | Fictional bar's beer, wine, sparkling, cocktail and zero-proof list (generic styles, example ABVs) |
| `guest-scripts.md` | 07.6 | Ten scripted guests with opening lines and scripted answers |
| `recommendation-sheet.md` | 07.6 | Answer template, 20-point rubric and the three safety-critical guests |
| `recommendation-model-answers-solution.md` | 07.6 | Model answers — open after writing all ten |

Templates used alongside: `templates/practice-log.md`, `templates/tasting-notes.md`.

Data: temperatures from the American Homebrewers Association table and the Brewers Association Draught Beer Quality Manual; wine service and temperatures from the Court of Master Sommeliers Europe Service Standards and WSET; Israeli wine regions from the 2026 map as reported by The Jerusalem Post and ice.co.il; alcohol-free definitions from GOV.UK and TTB. All checked 2026-10-17 and cited in the lessons. The drinks list and its ABVs are fictional examples, not product data. Targets (±10 g, time ranges, score bands) are professional judgment for practice.
