# Recommendation sheet and rubric (lesson 07.6)

Copy one block per guest.

```text
Guest #:
Question 1 (style):
Question 2 (strength / occasion):
Option A (item #, style, glass, temperature, ABV if relevant):
Option B (item #, style, glass, temperature, ABV if relevant):
What I say (one or two sentences):
Serve / opening notes:
Safety point (if any) and how I handled it:
```

## Rubric — 20 points

For each guest, 2 points:

| Point | Earned when |
|---|---|
| Fit (1) | both options fit the guest's words, food and occasion and are on the list |
| Service (1) | two sensible questions asked AND serve details right (glass, temperature, opening) AND ABV stated when it matters |

Score bands (professional judgment): 18–20 ready for real guests with supervision; 14–17 good, practise the guest types you missed; under 14 re-read lessons 07.1–07.5 and redo the exercise.

## Safety-critical guests

Guests 4, 9 and 10 are pass/fail on safety, whatever the total:

- **Guest 4:** only 0.0% options offered; any product above 0.0% named with its exact ABV, not recommended.
- **Guest 9:** no alcohol served or recommended; water, food or a zero-proof drink offered; manager informed.
- **Guest 10:** kosher red offered with the certification shown; mevushal checked on the label; non-mevushal bottle brought sealed with the guest asked how to open and pour.

A miss on any of these: re-read the lesson named in the model answer and redo that guest.
