# Grape-to-guest cards (lesson 07.3)

For each guest request, choose ONE of the eight principal grapes and write a one-line reason in guest language. Several answers can be defensible; the solution gives the best match and acceptable alternatives.

The eight: Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Syrah/Shiraz, Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, Sauvignon Blanc, Riesling, Pinot Grigio/Pinot Gris.
