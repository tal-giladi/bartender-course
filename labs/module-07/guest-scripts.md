# Ten scripted guests (lesson 07.6)

Read the opening line, write your two questions on the recommendation sheet, then read the scripted answers. Use only the drinks on `drinks-list.md`.

## Guest 1 — after work
- **Opening:** "Just a beer. Something light, I don't like bitter beers."
- **Scripted answers:** wants it on draught; plans to stay for one or two.

## Guest 2 — couple with grilled fish
- **Opening:** "We're having the grilled sea bream. A white, but not too sour, please."
- **Scripted answers:** they prefer "soft and round" to "crisp and zesty"; a glass each.

## Guest 3 — steak dinner
- **Opening:** "I want a big red for my entrecôte."
- **Scripted answers:** likes "strong, serious" reds; does not need it kosher.

## Guest 4 — birthday, pregnant guest
- **Opening:** "It's my birthday and I'm pregnant. Do you have something festive for me?"
- **Scripted answers:** wants "something in a nice glass, bubbly if possible"; no alcohol at all.

## Guest 5 — IPA fan
- **Opening:** "I always drink IPA. Give me something stronger and darker tonight."
- **Scripted answers:** walked in, not driving, is sober and chatty; one drink before dinner.

## Guest 6 — tourist
- **Opening:** "I'm visiting from Canada. What's something Israeli I should try?"
- **Scripted answers:** likes wine and is curious about anise; having dinner later.

## Guest 7 — celebration, group of six
- **Opening:** "We just got engaged! Bubbles for the table, please — something not too sweet."
- **Scripted answers:** one bottle for six, maybe a second; they want it opened at the table.

## Guest 8 — no alcohol, not sweet
- **Opening:** "I don't drink, but I hate sweet drinks. What can I have?"
- **Scripted answers:** likes bitter and sour flavours; avoids alcohol completely, for religious reasons.

## Guest 9 — late night
- **Opening:** (slurring, swaying, has spilled a drink at the bar) "Mate, give me your strongest beer. Come on."
- **Scripted answers:** insists politely; friends are with him.

## Guest 10 — observant guest
- **Opening:** "Do you have a kosher red? My friend is observant, and I know it matters who opens it."
- **Scripted answers:** they like "smooth" reds; four glasses or a bottle.
