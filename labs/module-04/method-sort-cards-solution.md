# Method sort cards — solution (lesson 04.1)

Methods are from the IBA official cocktail pages (https://iba-world.com/cocktails/), checked 2026-10-17. "Accepted alternative" means a method many bars use that still gives the drink its intended texture (professional judgment). Full specs come in Modules 5 and 6.

| # | Drink | IBA method | Deciding ingredient(s) | Accepted alternative |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Dry Martini | Stir in a mixing glass with ice, strain into a chilled glass | Spirits and wine only: needs chill and clarity, no bubbles | Throw (lesson 04.6) |
| 2 | Manhattan | Stir in a mixing glass with ice, strain into a chilled glass | Spirits, vermouth, bitters only | Throw |
| 3 | Daiquiri | Stir in the shaker to dissolve the sugar, then add ice, shake and strain | Citrus juice | — |
| 4 | Margarita | Shake with ice, strain into a chilled glass | Citrus juice | — |
| 5 | Cuba Libre | Build in a highball glass filled with ice | Carbonated mixer (cola) | — |
| 6 | Moscow Mule | Build in a mule cup or rocks glass, stir gently | Carbonated mixer (ginger beer) | — |
| 7 | Negroni | Build in a chilled old fashioned glass filled with ice, stir gently | Spirits and vermouth only | Stir in a mixing glass and strain onto fresh ice |
| 8 | Americano | Build in an old fashioned glass with ice, splash of soda, stir gently | Carbonated top | — |
| 9 | Spritz | Build in a wine glass filled with ice, stir gently | Sparkling wine and soda | — |
| 10 | Whiskey Sour | Shake with ice and strain; shake harder if using egg white | Citrus, optional egg white | Dry or reverse dry shake with egg white (lesson 04.4) |
| 11 | Pisco Sour | Shake with ice and strain into a chilled glass | Citrus and egg white | Dry or reverse dry shake |
| 12 | Clover Club | Shake well with ice, strain into a chilled glass | Citrus, syrup and egg white | Dry or reverse dry shake |
| 13 | Espresso Martini | Shake well with ice, strain into a chilled glass | Espresso: shaking builds the foam on top | — |
| 14 | White Lady | Shake well with ice, strain into a chilled glass | Citrus juice | — |

## The pattern

- Citrus, egg, cream, coffee or thick juices → shake (10, 11, 12, 13, 3, 4, 14).
- Only spirits, wines, liqueurs and bitters → stir (1, 2) or build and stir gently in the glass (7, 19).
- A carbonated mixer → build, carbonated last (5, 6, 8, 9).
- Fresh herbs or fruit pieces → muddle first, then build (15, 16, 17).
- A frozen, slushy texture → blend (18).
- Thick juices that should not foam → stir or roll instead of shaking hard (20).
