# Garnish tray checklist (lesson 04.7)

Tick each item after your garnish session. Targets are this course's practice standards (professional judgment), not legal limits; follow your bar's written food-safety policy where it differs.

## Hygiene

- [ ] Hands washed with soap for at least 20 seconds before prep.
- [ ] Fruit rinsed under running water and dried before cutting.
- [ ] Board on a damp cloth or non-slip mat; claw grip on every cut.
- [ ] Every garnish placed with tongs or picks, never bare fingers.
- [ ] Tray covered, labelled with the time, kept cold.

## Half salt rim

- [ ] Outside edge only, on half the glass.
- [ ] No salt inside the glass; loose salt tapped off.

## Clean-up

- [ ] Knife wiped from the spine side and stored; nothing left in the sink.
- [ ] Board and peeler washed; surface wiped.
- [ ] Unused cut garnish covered and refrigerated for use today, or discarded.
