# Method sort cards (lesson 04.1)

Twenty drinks, ingredients only, no amounts and no method. For each card, write the method you would choose (build, stir, shake, blend, muddle-then-build, or swizzle/churn) and one reason based on the ingredients. Then check your answers in `method-sort-cards-solution.md`, which gives the IBA method for each drink (checked 2026-10-17).

Print the page and cut along the lines, or copy the table into a spreadsheet and fill in the last two columns.
