# Technique drill sheet (lesson 04.8, module practical)

Ten reps per movement, filmed, timed and measured. Log every rep in `technique-drill-log.csv` (one row per rep; the 04.8 rows are already there). Then turn your numbers into rubric scores with the "Score" column below and fill in `templates/technique-rubric.md`.

Accuracy: error % = (measured − target) ÷ target × 100. In tolerance means within ±5% (the course standard from lessons 01.3 and 03.2). Water: 1 ml ≈ 1 g.

| Movement | Setup per rep | Target | Pass for one rep | Score (rubric 0–2) | Lesson | Written demonstration to compare |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Measuring (jigger) | Pour 30 ml water by jigger into a tared tin | 30 g, within 4 s | 28.5–31.5 g (±5%), no drips, jigger level | 2 = 10/10 pass, 1 = 7–9, 0 = fewer | 01.3, 03.2 | Lesson 01.3, "How to measure with a jigger" |

Safety-critical (course outline §6): scooping ice with a glass, leaving broken glass in the ice, or unsafe knife handling is a fail for the whole practical, whatever the total. Redo the drill after fixing it.
