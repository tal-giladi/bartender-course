# Complaint, substitution and unclear-order cases (lesson 09.2)

Write exactly what you would say (in quotation marks) and what you would do. Then compare with `complaint-cases-solution.md`.

1. A guest pushes back a Margarita you made to the house spec: "This is way too sour."
2. A guest's Aperol Spritz has been waiting at the pass for five minutes; when it arrives, she says it is flat and watery.
3. A guest finds a small chip on the rim of his highball glass after two sips.
4. A guest says his Old Fashioned is "too weak, I can hardly taste the whiskey. Can you add another shot, on the house?" He seems sober.
5. The Cointreau is finished and there is no backup. A guest orders a Cosmopolitan.
6. A guest orders "a Martini, sweet but very dry, and not too strong."
7. A tourist orders a "Mojito, but no mint, no sugar, and no soda."
8. A guest says: "We've been waiting twenty minutes for our drinks and that couple got served first. This is ridiculous. I want these for free."
