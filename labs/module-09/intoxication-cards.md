# Intoxication cards (lesson 09.4)

All guests and names are fictional. Answers are in `intoxication-cards-solution.md`.

## Part A — Green, amber or red?

For each guest: green, amber or red, and the one action you take next.

1. A couple sharing a bottle of wine over dinner for two hours; relaxed, speaking normally.
2. A man on his third Negroni in 40 minutes, getting louder, asks for "shots for the whole table".
3. A woman who arrived 10 minutes ago, steady and clear, orders her first drink; she mentions she had "a couple of beers" at a previous bar.
4. A guest who knocks over his glass, laughs, and asks you to repeat the price three times.
5. A birthday group: one member is sitting with her head on the table and does not answer her friends.
6. A guest who speaks slowly and walks with a stick; he has had one beer in an hour and is fully coherent.
7. A regular who usually has two beers is ordering his fifth, his speech is slightly slurred, and he leans on the bar.
8. A friend of a guest you refused 20 minutes ago orders "two gin and tonics".
9. A guest drinking fast to "catch up" with friends; he is cheerful and steady after two drinks.
10. A guest who has become aggressive, shouting at another guest after spilling a drink on himself.
11. A guest who says she is driving and asks for "just one small beer, I'm fine".
12. A quiet guest who has had four cocktails in an hour; she looks pale and has just vomited in the toilet, her friend says.

## Part B — Grams and standard drinks

Use grams = ml × ABV/100 × 0.789 and a 14 g standard drink. Round to one decimal.

1. A 500 ml beer at 5%.
2. A 175 ml glass of wine at 13%.
3. A Negroni: 30 ml gin 40%, 30 ml Campari (assume 25%), 30 ml sweet vermouth (assume 16%).
4. A double whisky: 60 ml at 40%.
5. A Mai Tai: 60 ml rum in total (assume 40%), 15 ml orange curaçao (assume 30%), plus non-alcoholic ingredients.

## Part C — Refusal situations (film yourself)

1. Avi, a regular, slurring and swaying, asks for one more round and shots for everyone.
2. A guest you refused 20 minutes ago comes back: "I've had water, I'm fine now, just one beer."
3. A group orders a round of six tequila shots; one of them is clearly unsteady and has just dropped his phone twice.
