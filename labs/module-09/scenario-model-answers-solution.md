# Scenario model answers (lesson 09.7)

Examples that would score 10/10 with the rubric. Your wording can differ; the choices and the rule should not. Legal references checked 2026-10-18.

## 1. Possible minor

"Hi guys! Can I see ID from both of you, please?" To the phone photo: "Sorry, I need to see the actual card. Without it I can't serve alcohol; it's the law. I can make you something without alcohol: a lemonade with mint, or a ginger beer?" Serve the friend one beer only if he shows his own valid ID, and only for him; if you see the beer passed to the young guest, take it back politely and tell the manager. If the young man argues, call the manager. Log the ID refusal if the house asks.
Rule: Penal Law s. 193A(a1)/(b), presumption (c1); licence condition: ID required to drink (statutory + licence condition).

## 2. Intoxicated guest

Tell the manager. Privately, at the end of the bar: "Avi, I can't pour you any more alcohol tonight. It's the law and our policy, and I want you to get home safe. Let me get you water and something to eat, and I'll call you a taxi." No shots for the table that would include him. About the keys: "Leave the car tonight; I'll call the taxi now." If he insists on driving, the manager decides on calling the police (100). Keep watching him; if he becomes unwell, do not leave him alone and call 101 for signs of alcohol poisoning. Write it in the incident log.
Rule: Penal Law s. 193(c) (statutory); signs and management from official guidance.

## 3. Unclear order

"A Martini can go a couple of ways. Dry means less vermouth and more spirit, which makes it stronger. Would you like a softer vodka Martini with more vermouth, a bit sweeter and lighter, or something fruity and sweet like a French Martini?" Guest chooses. "One softer vodka Martini with extra vermouth, stirred and served up. Perfect." Make it, serve, check in.
Rule: clarification method (professional judgment).

## 4. Allergy

"Thanks for telling me. Which nuts, and is a trace a problem? The Mai Tai has orgeat, which is an almond syrup, so I can't make that one for you." Check the labels of any alternative's ingredients. "I can make you a Daiquiri or a Mojito; I'll use a freshly washed shaker and fresh ice. The rums and lime are nut-free by their labels." Wash hands, clean set, fresh ice, garnish from a closed container. Mark the ticket "nut allergy". Serve it yourself and check in. If you cannot confirm a product: say so and choose another.
Rule: allergen list and labels (EU 1169/2011 adopted in Israel; MoH guide 2025); never guess (professional standard).

## 5. Complaint

Listen. "I'm sorry it's not how you like it. I can make it a little sweeter, or make you something else, maybe a Paloma?" Guest wants it sweeter. Bin it, remake with about +5 ml syrup in a clean glass, taste-check with a straw, serve: "Here you go, a touch sweeter. Thanks for telling me." Check in after a few sips. Log the waste.
Rule: listen, apologise, solve, thank (professional judgment).

## 6. Strong drink request

Check for signs of intoxication: none. "We pour the same measure in every drink, so no triples, sorry. If you like strong, a Negroni or a Martini is about as spirit-forward as it gets, and I can make it with a double measure, charged as a double." Do not describe it as a way to get drunk fast; offer water with it. Keep an eye on his pace; at any sign of intoxication, no more alcohol.
Rule: house measures (house policy); s. 193(c) if he becomes intoxicated (statutory).

## 7. Alcohol-free request

"Congratulations! I can make you something festive with no alcohol at all. How about a sparkling grapefruit and rosemary highball in a flute, or the 0.0% sparkling wine with a little elderflower syrup?" Do not add Angostura (about 44.7% ABV) or offer the 0.5% beer as "alcohol-free"; if she asks about it, say "it has 0.5% alcohol, so I'd suggest the 0.0% options". Serve it in a nice glass with a proper garnish so it looks like a real cocktail.
Rule: Ministry of Health: avoid alcohol completely in pregnancy (official guidance); state the ABV of anything that is not 0.0% (lesson 07.5).
