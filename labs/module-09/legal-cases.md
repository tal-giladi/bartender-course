# Legal cases (lesson 09.5)

For each case write: allowed / not allowed / house policy decides; the rule; and whether it is statutory, a licence condition or house policy. Answers in `legal-cases-solution.md`. All people and venues are fictional. This is a learning exercise, not legal advice (research date 2026-10-18).

1. A father at the bar orders two beers, one for himself and one for his 16-year-old son sitting next to him.
2. A guest who looks about 20 shows a photo of her ID card on her phone; she says the card is at home.
3. It is 23:30. A guest at your bar asks to buy a closed bottle of wine to take home.
4. It is 23:30. A guest sitting at your bar orders a glass of wine.
5. Your bar has a "we ID anyone who looks under 30" rule. A 35-year-old regular is offended when a new colleague asks him for ID.
6. A 17-year-old applies for a barback job: carrying ice and glasses, and selling drinks when it is busy.
7. A guest lights a cigarette at the bar inside. The bar has a separate, enclosed, ventilated smoking room.
8. You put an ashtray on the bar for a regular "just this once" because it is late and quiet.
9. A guest orders a 0.0% beer for her 15-year-old daughter at dinner.
10. A group asks you to give their friend, who turned 18 yesterday, a free birthday shot. He shows a valid ID.
11. The minors sign fell down last week and is in the storeroom.
12. A guest who is clearly drunk asks his sober friend to order him a beer; the friend orders two.
