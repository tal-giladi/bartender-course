# Module 9 labs — Customer Service, Responsible Service and Israeli Requirements

Worksheets, cards and printables for the Module 9 hands-on work and the module's practical piece (lesson 09.7: seven guest scenarios in the simulation plus written responses scored with a rubric). No alcohol is needed; the optional drink-building parts use water, citrus, syrups and soda.

| File | Lesson | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `order-taking-drill.md` | 09.1 | Six greeting situations and ten orders with modifiers for a partner to read (answers at the bottom) |
| `complaint-cases.md` | 09.2 | Eight complaint, substitution and unclear-order cases |
| `complaint-cases-solution.md` | 09.2 | Model answers |
| `allergen-matrix.csv` | 09.3 | Twenty classic drinks with spec ingredients and empty allergen columns |
| `allergen-worksheet.md` | 09.3 | Instructions, label-check table, clean-set drill log |
| `allergen-matrix-solution.md` | 09.3 | Reference allergens per drink and items to check on labels |
| `intoxication-cards.md` | 09.4 | Twelve guest descriptions (green/amber/red), five grams-of-alcohol calculations, three refusal situations |
| `intoxication-cards-solution.md` | 09.4 | Reference answers |
| `incident-log.md` | 09.4–09.7 | Printable incident log for refusals, ID refusals, allergy incidents and injuries |
| `legal-cases.md` | 09.5 | Twelve legal cases and cut-off dates |
| `id-check-cards.md` | 09.5 | Ten fictional ID descriptions for a timed drill |
| `legal-cases-solution.md` | 09.5 | Answers with the rule and its type, cut-off dates, ID drill decisions |
| `safety-self-audit.md` | 09.6 | Twenty-point home-station audit against licence conditions and course rules |
| `scenario-response-sheet.md` | 09.7 | Module practical: the seven situations, space for written responses, summary table |
| `scenario-rubric.md` | 09.7, 11.5 | Five-criterion rubric and the safety-critical choices per situation |
| `scenario-model-answers-solution.md` | 09.7 | Model answers for the seven situations |

Simulation: `guest-scenarios` presets `complaint`, `unclear` (09.2), `allergy` (09.3), `intoxicated`, `strong` (09.4), `underage` (09.5), all seven including `alcohol-free` (09.7).

Sources and dates: Israeli law from Nevo consolidated texts (Penal Law to 2 July 2026; Licensing of Businesses Law to 21 July 2026; Smoking Law to 8 December 2023), the police and Ministry of Health licence conditions in the uniform specifications for licensing items 4.8 and 4.2A on gov.il, the Ministry of Health allergen guide (29 June 2025) and Kovetz HaTakanot 12471 (14 July 2026); all checked 2026-10-18. Specs as in Modules 5 and 6 (IBA). Guests, names, venues and ID cards are fictional. Nothing here is legal advice.
