# Order-taking drill (lesson 09.1)

For a partner who plays the guest. Read each card at normal speed, once. The learner checks it back; answer "yes" or correct them. Answers are at the bottom; the learner should not look until scoring.

## Part A — Greeting situations

Read the situation; the learner acknowledges, then greets when free (English and Hebrew).

1. You are shaking two Daiquiris. Two guests sit down at the far end of the bar.
2. You are taking an order from a couple. A man waves a card at you from behind them.
3. The bar is quiet. A woman walks in alone and looks around for a seat.
4. Two groups reach the bar at the same moment; you did not see who was first.
5. A regular guest arrives while you are counting the till.
6. A family with a teenager sits down at the bar to wait for a table.

## Part B — Orders

Read with music on, from a couple of metres away.

1. "Two Aperol Spritzes and a pint of the lager, please. One spritz without the orange slice."
2. "Can I get a Moscow Mule, but with the alcohol-free ginger beer… no, sorry, the normal ginger beer, and make it a double."
3. "Negroni on the rocks for me, and he'll have the same but up."
4. "A gin and tonic with the pink gin, slimline tonic, no ice. On my tab, seat 4."
5. "Three Margaritas: two with salt, one without, and one of the salted ones on the rocks."
6. "An Old Fashioned with rye instead of bourbon, and a glass of tap water."
7. "Two Mojitos, one less sweet, and a Diet Coke with lemon, not lime."
8. "I'm allergic to nuts. Can I have a Mai Tai?" (The correct response is not to make it: see lesson 09.3.)
9. "What do you recommend?" If asked: "Something fresh and citrusy", then "long and light, I'm driving later." (Learner should offer alcohol-free options.)
10. "What do you recommend?" If asked: "Rich and spirit-forward", then "short and strong is fine, it's my only drink tonight."

---

## Answers (for scoring)

Part A, model actions: (1) eye contact and "One minute!" while shaking; greet when the Daiquiris are served. (2) Nod to the man ("I'll be right with you"), finish the couple's order. (3) Greet at once, offer a seat and a menu. (4) Ask "Who was first?" and serve in that order. (5) Stop counting, lock the till, greet. (6) Greet everyone, offer menus and water; anything with alcohol for the teenager is refused, and ID checks follow lesson 09.5.

Part B, correct check-backs:

1. Two Aperol Spritzes, one with no orange slice; one pint of lager.
2. One Moscow Mule, double vodka, regular ginger beer (the guest corrected themselves: check back to confirm which).
3. Two Negronis: one on the rocks, one up.
4. One pink-gin and slimline tonic, no ice, seat 4's tab.
5. Three Margaritas: one salted on the rocks, one salted up, one unsalted up. (If the guest did not say "up" for the other two, ask: the house serve may be up.)
6. One Old Fashioned with rye; one glass of tap water.
7. Two Mojitos, one less sweet; one Diet Coke with lemon.
8. Do not make the Mai Tai (orgeat is almond syrup). Ask which nuts, explain, and offer a drink you can confirm is nut-free (lesson 09.3).
9. Two alcohol-free options, for example a zero-proof highball and a 0.0% beer; state the ABV of anything that is not 0.0% (lesson 07.5).
10. Two stirred drinks you make well, for example a Manhattan or a Boulevardier; one line each.

Scoring: 1 point per order correct after check-back (10 total). Target 10/10.
