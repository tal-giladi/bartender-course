## Scoring

One point per cell (4 per drink, 48 in total). A cell scores only if it is fully right:

- **Ingredients and ml:** every ingredient named, every ml within ±5 ml; dashes, cubes, sprigs, bar spoons and "to top" as the spec says.
- **Method:** the right technique and its key step (dissolve sugar, dry shake for egg, top with soda last).
- **Glass and ice:** the right glass and the ice in it, or "no ice".
- **Garnish:** the spec garnish ("none" for the IBA Daiquiri).
