# Module 5 spec flashcards

Print this page, cut along the lines, and fold each card so the name is on the front and the spec on the back. Writing your own cards teaches more; use these to check them. All specs are IBA, checked on iba-world.com on 2026-10-17. 1 oz = 30 ml (bar convention).

Score a card as passed only if every ml (±5 ml), the method, the glass and ice, and the garnish are right.

---

**Dry Martini** · stirred template
Gin 60 ml · dry vermouth 10 ml.
Stir with ice, strain. Chilled martini glass, no ice.
Garnish: lemon peel oil squeezed over the drink, or green olive if requested.

---

**Manhattan** · stirred template
Rye whiskey 50 ml · sweet red vermouth 20 ml · Angostura bitters 1 dash.
Stir with ice, strain. Chilled cocktail glass, no ice.
Garnish: cocktail cherry.

---

**Old Fashioned** · stirred template
Bourbon or rye 45 ml · 1 sugar cube · few dashes Angostura · few dashes plain water.
Soak the cube with bitters and water, muddle until dissolved, fill with ice, add whiskey, stir gently. Old fashioned glass, ice cubes.
Garnish: orange slice or zest, and a cocktail cherry.

---

**Negroni** · stirred template
Gin 30 ml · Campari 30 ml · sweet red vermouth 30 ml.
Build in a chilled old fashioned glass filled with ice, stir gently.
Garnish: half orange slice.

---

**Daiquiri** · sour template
White rum 60 ml · fresh lime juice 20 ml · 2 bar spoons superfine sugar.
Stir in the shaker to dissolve the sugar, add ice, shake, strain. Chilled cocktail glass, no ice.
Garnish: none in the IBA spec.

---

**Margarita** · sour template
Tequila 100% agave 50 ml · triple sec 20 ml · fresh lime juice 15 ml.
Shake with ice, strain. Chilled cocktail glass, no ice.
Garnish: half salt rim (optional).

---

**Whiskey Sour** · sour template
Bourbon 45 ml · fresh lemon juice 25 ml · sugar syrup 20 ml · few drops egg white (optional).
Shake with ice, strain. Cobbler glass, or old fashioned glass with ice for "on the rocks".
Garnish: half orange slice and maraschino cherry (orange zest optional).

---

**Cosmopolitan** · sour template
Citrus vodka 40 ml · Cointreau 15 ml · fresh lime juice 15 ml · cranberry juice 30 ml.
Shake with ice, strain. Large cocktail glass, no ice.
Garnish: lemon twist.

---

**Mojito** · highball template
White rum 45 ml · fresh lime juice 20 ml · 6 mint sprigs · 2 tsp white cane sugar · soda water to top.
Muddle mint with sugar and lime, add a splash of soda, fill with ice, add rum, top with soda, stir lightly. Highball glass, ice.
Garnish: mint sprigs and a lime slice.

---

**Moscow Mule** · highball template
Vodka 45 ml · ginger beer 120 ml · fresh lime juice 10 ml.
Build vodka and ginger beer in the mug or glass with ice, add lime, stir gently. Mule cup or rocks glass, ice.
Garnish: lime slice.

---

**Aperol Spritz** (IBA "Spritz") · sparkling template
Prosecco 90 ml · Aperol 60 ml · splash of soda water.
Build in a wine glass filled with ice, stir gently.
Garnish: orange slice.

---

**Espresso Martini** · coffee template
Vodka 50 ml · coffee liqueur (Kahlúa) 30 ml · sugar syrup 10 ml · 1 strong espresso.
Shake hard with ice, strain. Chilled cocktail glass, no ice.
Garnish: 3 coffee beans.
