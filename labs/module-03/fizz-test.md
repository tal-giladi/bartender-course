# Fizz test worksheet (lesson 03.6)

Non-alcoholic. Same recipe in both glasses: 22.5 ml lemon juice + 15 ml 1:1 syrup + about 150 ml soda water.
Wipe the outside of each glass dry before every weighing: cold glasses collect water from the air.
