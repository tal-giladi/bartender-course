# Unbalanced drinks — fault cards and scoring (lesson 03.7, module practical)

Non-alcoholic. Your **partner** reads this page; you (the taster) read only the scoring sheet at the bottom until all six diagnoses are written.

## Reference drinks (the taster makes these first)

- **Practice sour:** 60 ml cold black tea (1 bag, 200 ml water, brewed 4 minutes, cooled), 22.5 ml lemon juice, 22.5 ml 1:1 syrup. Shake 15 s with 150 g ice cubes, strain into a glass.
- **Practice highball:** glass filled with ice cubes, 22.5 ml lemon juice, 15 ml 1:1 syrup, 150 ml cold soda water poured gently, one lift.

## Fault cards (partner: cut out, shuffle, make each exactly as written, number glasses 1–6 underneath, note which card is which number)

**Card A.** Practice sour with **40 ml syrup** instead of 22.5 ml. Shake and strain normally.

**Card B.** Practice sour with **35 ml lemon juice** instead of 22.5 ml. Shake and strain normally.

**Card C.** Practice sour shaken for **only 3 seconds** with 4 ice cubes. Strain at once.

**Card D.** Practice sour shaken 15 s with **crushed ice** that has sat in a bowl for 10 minutes, then **left in the tin on the ice for 2 minutes** before straining.

**Card E.** Practice highball made with **room-temperature soda** poured from 20 cm onto **three cubes and crushed ice**, then **stirred round ten times**. Serve after 3 minutes.

**Card F.** Practice sour made with tea from **2 bags brewed 12 minutes**. Shake and strain normally.

For cards A–D and F, weigh the mix before adding ice and tell the taster that weight.
