# Scaling worksheet (lesson 03.1)

Use this course's values: 1 oz = 30 ml (bar convention), 1 cl = 10 ml, 1 barspoon = 5 ml, 1 dash = 0.8 ml. Show every calculation. Round only the final answers (to 0.5 ml, or to the nearest ¼ oz). Check your answers in `scaling-worksheet-solution.md` only after you finish.

## Conversions

1. A recipe asks for 2 oz. How many ml?
2. A recipe asks for ¾ oz. How many ml?
3. An old European card says 4.5 cl. How many ml?
4. A metric spec gives 50 ml. What is that in bar ounces, to the nearest ¼ oz?
5. How many ml are 3 dashes plus 2 barspoons?

## Parts

6. A drink is 2 : 1 : 1 (base : sour : sweet). Make it 120 ml in total. Give each ingredient in ml.
7. A drink is 1½ : ¾ : ½ parts. Make it 110 ml in total. Give each ingredient in ml.

## Scaling and batching

8. One drink: 45 ml base, 20 ml lemon, 15 ml syrup, 1 dash bitters. Scale it for 12 drinks. Give each line and the total.
9. A stirred drink: 50 ml spirit, 25 ml aperitif, 25 ml vermouth. Batch for 8, served from a cold bottle without ice-mixing, planned dilution 30%. How much water? What is the batch total and one serving?
10. You batch 20 practice sours (60 ml tea, 22.5 ml lemon, 22.5 ml syrup) that will be shaken on ice one at a time during service. How much water do you add, and what is the batch total?
