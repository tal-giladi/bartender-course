# Free-pour and syrup drill (lesson 03.2)

Water in a bottle with a speed pourer; weigh every pour on a scale with 1 g steps (1 ml water ≈ 1 g). Tare before each pour.

Tolerance ±5% (±1 g for 15 ml, because the scale shows whole grams).

| Target | Count (start) | In tolerance on the scale |
|---|---|---|
| 15 ml water | 2 | 14–16 g |
| 30 ml water | 4 | 29–31 g |
| 45 ml water | 6 | 43–47 g |
| 22.5 ml 1:1 syrup (jigger) | — | 27–29 g (22.5 × 1.23 = 27.7 g) |

Error % = (measured − target) ÷ target × 100. For syrup, convert grams to ml first: ml = g ÷ 1.23.
