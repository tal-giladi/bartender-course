# Scaling worksheet — solution (lesson 03.1)

Check only after answering. Values: 1 oz = 30 ml, 1 cl = 10 ml, barspoon 5 ml, dash 0.8 ml.

1. 2 × 30 = **60 ml**.
2. 0.75 × 30 = **22.5 ml** (some metric specs write 20 or 25 ml).
3. 4.5 × 10 = **45 ml**.
4. 50 ÷ 30 = 1.67 oz → nearest ¼ oz = **1¾ oz**.
5. 3 × 0.8 + 2 × 5 = 2.4 + 10 = **12.4 ml**.
6. Sum = 4 parts; 120 ÷ 4 = 30 ml per part → **60 ml base, 30 ml sour, 30 ml sweet**.
7. Sum = 1.5 + 0.75 + 0.5 = 2.75 parts; 110 ÷ 2.75 = 40 ml per part → **60 ml, 30 ml, 20 ml**.
8. × 12: base **540 ml**, lemon **240 ml**, syrup **180 ml**, bitters 12 × 0.8 = **9.6 ml** (about 12 dashes). Total **969.6 ml** (call it 970 ml; a 1-litre bottle is just enough, so use a bigger container to leave room to mix).
9. Undiluted: (50 + 25 + 25) × 8 = 800 ml. Water: 800 × 0.30 = **240 ml**. Batch total **1,040 ml**. One serving 1,040 ÷ 8 = **130 ml**.
10. **No water**: the ice adds it when each serve is shaken. Batch total: (60 + 22.5 + 22.5) × 20 = **2,100 ml**.

Typical mistakes: multiplying ounces by 29.57 and then rounding differently from the card (fine for one drink, inconsistent for a team); forgetting the bitters in the total (problem 8); adding water in problem 10.
