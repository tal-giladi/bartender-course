# Guest scenarios exam — written cases (lesson 11.5)

Three new situations, close to but not the same as the Module 9 scenarios. Closed book, 30 minutes in total. For each case write exactly what you would **say** (in quotation marks) and **do**, step by step, and name the rule behind your main decision. Then score each response with the [scenario rubric](../module-09/scenario-rubric.md) and compare with `guest-exam-cases-solution.md`.

## Case A — the birthday table

Thursday, 21:30. A table of five orders a round: four pints of lager and "a mojito for the birthday girl". Four of them show Israeli ID cards when you ask; all are over 18. The birthday girl looks about 17. She says her ID is in her friend's car, shows you a student card from a university abroad with no date of birth, and her friend says "Relax, she's my cousin, she's 20."

## Case B — one for the road

Saturday, 01:15. A guest who has been at the bar since 22:00 (five beers and two shots, by your tab) asks for "one last whisky for the road". His speech has become loud and slurred, he knocked over a glass ten minutes ago, and his car keys are on the bar next to his phone. He is friendly and says he lives "only ten minutes away".

## Case C — the egg allergy

A guest orders a Pisco Sour and adds, "I'm allergic to eggs, is that a problem?" Your bar's Pisco Sour uses pasteurised egg white from a carton; you also have a closed can of chickpeas and a tin of aquafaba made this afternoon in the same kitchen as the bar's egg-white cocktails. Your only clean shaker set is at the other end of the bar.
