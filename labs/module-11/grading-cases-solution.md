## Case 1 — Noa

- Written = 0.50 × 70 + 0.25 × 76 + 0.25 × 64 = 35 + 19 + 16 = **70.0%** (exactly the minimum: met).
- Recall 65 ÷ 80 = 81.25%; technique 15 ÷ 18 = 83.3%; service 82%; costing 30 ÷ 40 = 75%.
- Weighted: 70 × 0.20 = 14.00; 81.25 × 0.25 = 20.31; 83.3 × 0.25 = 20.83; 82 × 0.20 = 16.40; 75 × 0.10 = 7.50. Total **79.0**.
- Every minimum met, no safety failure, but the total is below 80.0. **Not passed yet.** Cheapest route: recall carries 25% and is close to its ceiling with practice; raising it to 72 / 80 (90%) adds 2.2 points and passes. A retest of the written part would also help, since its quiz part is weak.
