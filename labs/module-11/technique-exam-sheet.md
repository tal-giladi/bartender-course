# Practical technique exam — exam set and evidence sheet (lesson 11.3)

Two rounds of the same exam set, filmed, in one session. Water, tea, juice and syrup only. Score with the course [technique rubric](../../templates/technique-rubric.md) using the evidence rules below. Water: 1 ml ≈ 1 g. Accuracy: error % = (measured − target) ÷ target × 100.

## Filming

- Phone on a stand, landscape, showing your hands, the jigger, the tins, the glass and the scale display in one frame.
- One continuous take per round. Say the round number and the time at the start.
- Keep the files until you have finished lesson 11.7 (and longer if a reviewer will watch them).

## The exam set (each round)

| Step | Task | Home-practice version | Standard |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Measuring | Four jigger pours into a tared tin on the scale: 30, 30, 15, 15 ml water | each within ±5% (30 ml: 28.5–31.5 g; 15 ml: 14.3–15.7 g), no drips, jigger level |
| 2 | Build | Highball with cubes: 45 ml cold tea, 10 ml lemon or lime juice, 120 ml soda or ginger ale, one gentle lift; weigh the tea pour | order glass → ice → tea → juice → soda → lift; tea 45 g ±5% |
| 3 | Stir | Mixing glass two-thirds ice, 70 ml cold tea and water; stir 20–30 s; julep or Hawthorne strain into a chilled coupe | spoon back on the glass, quiet, no splash; time on the stopwatch |
| 4 | Shake | Large tin two-thirds ice, 60 ml water, 20 ml lime or lemon juice, 15 ml 1:1 syrup; seal, shake 10–15 s, open | no leak, two hands, safe grip, tins open in under 3 s |
| 5 | Strain | Double strain the shaken drink into a coupe | correct strainers, no ice chips, no drips down the glass |
| 6 | Muddle | Highball: 3 lime wedges and 10 ml syrup pressed 4–6 times; 6 mint leaves pressed gently; ice, soda top, stir lightly | pressing not shredding, leaves whole, glass held at the base (use a tin or sturdy glass, never thin glass) |
| 7 | Garnish | Cut, express and place a lemon or orange twist on the stirred drink; a lime slice on the highball | twist ≤ 20 s, 1.5–2 cm wide, oil visible, consistent placement |
| 8 | Free pour (optional) | Two 4-count pours of 30 ml with a speed pourer, weighed | within ±10% (27–33 g) |
| 9 | Station reset | After the round: tools rinsed and back, surface wiped, bottles capped, cloth folded | ≤ 90 s, nothing left wet or out of place |
| 10 | Safety | Throughout | scoop for ice, knife away from the hand on a board on a damp cloth, glass checked for chips, nothing breakable near the tins |

## Converting evidence to rubric scores

| Rubric line | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Measuring (jigger) | 8 / 8 pours in tolerance | 6–7 / 8 | 5 or fewer |
| Free pour (optional) | 4 / 4 | 3 / 4 | 2 or fewer |
| Build, Stir, Shake, Strain, Muddle, Garnish | both rounds meet the standard | one round | neither round |
| Station reset | both resets ≤ 90 s and complete | one | neither |
| Safety | no fault | one minor fault corrected at once (for example a bottle left uncapped near the tins) | a minor fault repeated |

Total: ____ / 20 (or ____ / 18 without free pour) = ____ %. Minimum 75% (15 / 20 or 14 / 18).

## Safety-critical check (any one fails this part, whatever the total)

- [ ] I never scooped ice with a glass.
- [ ] No glass broke near the ice, or if it did, I stopped and burned the ice before continuing.
- [ ] Every cut was away from my hand, on a stable board, with the knife put down flat and away from the edge.
- [ ] I used no raw egg (aquafaba or pasteurised egg white only, if I added a foam).

If any box is unticked: remediation (lessons 01.4, 01.6 and 04.5 as relevant, plus a week of drills) and a full retake of this exam set.
