# Substitution worksheet — solution (lesson 06.6)

Model answers. Other wordings are fine if the decision is the same. Amounts follow the course cards (IBA unless stated), checked 2026-10-17.

| # | What you make | What you tell the guest | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | IBA Daiquiri with about 10 ml 2:1 syrup instead of 2 bar spoons sugar (2 bar spoons ≈ 15 ml 1:1 ≈ 10 ml 2:1). | Nothing needed: same balance, same drink. | Same role (sweet), adjusted to ⅔ volume for 2:1 (03.4, 05.4). |
| 2 | Sidecar spec (50 brandy / 20 triple sec / 20 lemon) with the Spanish brandy. | "We're out of cognac; I can make it with a Spanish brandy, slightly different character. Is that OK?" | Same role, but the name promises cognac; honesty before serving. |
| 3 | The IBA Collins (45 gin / 30 lemon / 15 syrup / 60 soda, built on ice) with London dry gin; optionally 5 ml more syrup. | If asked: "Made with London dry gin; the IBA calls that a John Collins." | IBA note: Old Tom makes it a Tom Collins; most bars use London dry. |
| 4 | Pisco Sour with 30 ml aquafaba instead of egg white; check the label of the bitters and every other product for animal ingredients. | "It's made with aquafaba, from chickpeas, instead of egg." | Aquafaba 1:1 for egg white; the guest must know about chickpea. |
| 5 | Not a Mai Tai. Offer another rum sour without nuts (e.g. a Daiquiri) made with clean tools. | "The Mai Tai's orgeat is made from almonds, so I'd suggest a Daiquiri instead." | Any amount of almond is a risk; without orgeat it is not a Mai Tai. |
| 6 | A half-strength Whiskey Sour (about 22.5 ml bourbon, 25 lemon, 20 syrup, shaken), or the same lengthened with soda; or an alcohol-free sour. Remind them that any alcohol affects driving. | "I can do a half-strength one, or an alcohol-free version. If you're driving, the alcohol-free one is the safe choice." | Lower-alcohol adaptation; responsible service (Module 9). |
| 7 | Ask first. | "Chilled and strained with no ice, or neat at room temperature? And gin or vodka?" | "Straight up" is used for both "up" and "neat"; "Martini" has several meanings. |
| 8 | Alcohol-free French 75-style: 30 ml strong juniper or black tea, 15 ml lemon, 15–20 ml 1:1 syrup, shaken, topped with alcohol-free sparkling wine or soda; no bitters. | "This one is completely alcohol-free; every product in it is 0.0%." | Body ingredient replaces the gin; check every product, including the sparkling wine, is alcohol-free. |
