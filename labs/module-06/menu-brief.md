## 6. Rubric (self-scored, 20 points; pass 14)

| Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Families | ≤ 4 | 5 | ≥ 6 |
| Methods and speed | 1–2 methods or > 6 shaken | 3 methods, 6 shaken | ≥ 3 methods, ≤ 5 shaken, ≤ 1 foamed |
| Strength range | < 2 lighter drinks | 2 | ≥ 3 |
| Base spirit variety | ≤ 2 | 3 | ≥ 4 |
| Spec source on every row | missing on ≥ 3 | missing on 1–2 | all rows |
| Allergen note on every row | missing on ≥ 3 | missing on 1–2 | all rows |
| Alcohol-free drinks | none | 1, or 2 without full cards | ≥ 2 with full recipe cards |
| Shared ingredients / prep list | no prep list | prep list, ≥ 2 single-use items | prep list, ≤ 1 single-use item |
| Glassware | ≥ 6 types | 5 | ≤ 4 |
| Local or sourced story | none | local item, no source | local item with a named source |

This score is self-assessed and unverified. If you can, ask a working bartender to look at your menu and tell you what they would change.
