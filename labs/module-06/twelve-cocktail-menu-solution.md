## Guest-facing order

Fresh and sour: Daiquiri · Gin Basil Smash · Penicillin · Mai Tai
Long and fizzy: Paloma · Tom Collins · Hugo Spritz · Arak and grapefruit
Stirred and strong: Old Fashioned · Negroni
Brunch and after dinner: Bloody Mary · Espresso Martini
No alcohol: Virgin Mary · Grapefruit-basil soda

Rubric self-score: 19/20 (one point lost: two single-use prep items).
