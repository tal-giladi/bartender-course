# Pricing worksheet (lesson 10.2)

VAT 18% (Israel, since 1 January 2025). Costs are ex VAT, from lesson 10.1 and `price-list.csv`. Round menu prices **up** to the next 2 ILS. Formulas:

- net price = menu price ÷ 1.18
- pour cost % = cost ÷ net price × 100
- gross profit = net price − cost
- margin = gross profit ÷ net price × 100; markup = gross profit ÷ cost × 100
- price for a target: cost ÷ target × 1.18, rounded up
