# Cost per serving worksheet — solution (lesson 10.1)

Numbers computed with the same formulas as the costing calculator (`simulations/common/bar-math.js`). Small differences (± 0.02) from rounding are fine.

## Part A

| Item | Ex VAT | Cost per unit |
|---|---|---|
| Campari | 105.90 ÷ 1.18 = 89.75 | 89.75 ÷ 700 = 0.1282 per ml |
| Sweet vermouth | 64.90 ÷ 1.18 = 55.00 | 55.00 ÷ 750 = 0.0733 per ml |
| Lemons | 12.90 ÷ 1.18 = 10.93 per kg | 10.93 ÷ 300 = 0.0364 per ml of juice |

## Part B

| Drink | Lines | Cost per serving |
|---|---|---|
| Negroni | gin 30 × 0.1282 ÷ 0.97 = 3.97; Campari 3.97; vermouth 30 × 0.0733 ÷ 0.97 = 2.27; orange 0.40 | **10.60** |
| Bloody Mary | vodka 45 × 0.0846 ÷ 0.97 = 3.93; tomato 90 × 0.0102 ÷ 0.95 = 0.96; lemon 15 × 0.0364 ÷ 0.90 = 0.61; seasoning 0.50; celery 0.50 | **6.50** |
| Arak and grapefruit | arak 60 × 0.0786 ÷ 0.97 = 4.86; grapefruit 6.69 ÷ 400 = 0.0167 per ml, 120 × 0.0167 ÷ 0.90 = 2.23; basil 0.0669 ÷ 0.80 = 0.08 | **7.18** |

Biggest line in each: gin and Campari (Negroni), vodka (Bloody Mary), arak (Arak and grapefruit). The grapefruit is almost a third of the arak drink's cost, because 120 ml of juice is a lot of fruit.

## Part C

1. 700 × 0.97 = 679 ÷ 45 = 15.1 → **15**
2. 679 ÷ 30 = 22.6 → **22**
3. 750 × 0.97 = 727.5 ÷ 60 = 12.1 → **12**
4. 1,000 × 0.97 = 970 ÷ 60 = 16.2 → **16**

## Part D (example)

520 g of lemons at 12.90 ILS per kg incl. VAT gave 158 ml: yield 158 ÷ 0.52 = 304 ml per kg; 10.93 ÷ 304 = 0.0360 ILS per ml. Your own result will differ; use it in lesson 10.5.
