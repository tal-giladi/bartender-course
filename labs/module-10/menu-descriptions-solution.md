# Menu descriptions — model answers (lesson 10.4)

Your wording will differ; check that yours names the base spirit, the main flavours, the strength or texture, and every allergen, and promises nothing the recipe does not deliver.

| # | Problem | Model description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Misleading ("light": it holds 60 ml of rum) and unclear (no ingredients); nut allergen hidden | Tropical Sunset — two rums, lime, orange curaçao and almond syrup (contains nuts). Sweet-sour and strong, on crushed ice. |
| 2 | Misleading: names a brand that is not poured (section 2: producer, trade name) | Moscow Mule — vodka, lime and spicy ginger beer over ice. Long and refreshing. |
| 3 | Misleading: "fresh" while the lime juice is bottled. Either squeeze fresh lime or drop the word | Margarita — tequila, triple sec and lime, shaken and served straight up. Sharp and citrusy, medium-strong. |
| 4 | Sells strength and heavy drinking; unclear what it is | The Knockout (rename it, for example "Boulevardier") — bourbon, Campari and sweet vermouth, stirred. Bitter-sweet and strong. |
| 5 | Misleading: "Israeli gin" and "local herbs" are not true (section 2: place of production) | Garden Smash — gin, lemon and fresh basil, shaken. Bright and herbal, medium strength. |
| 6 | Allergen missing: egg white | Whiskey Sour — bourbon, lemon and sugar, shaken with pasteurised egg white (contains egg) for a silky foam. |
| 7 | "0%" is not accurate if the label says under 0.5% | Alcohol-free Spritz — bitter orange aperitif and alcohol-free sparkling wine with soda. Contains traces of alcohol (under 0.5%). |
| 8 | Unclear ("classic") and allergens missing: fish (anchovies in Worcestershire), celery | Bloody Mary — vodka, tomato, lemon and spices. Savoury and peppery. Contains fish and celery. |

Notes:

- Check the label of every product you list; Worcestershire sauces and NA products differ by brand.
- Whether a drink with under 0.5% may be called "alcohol-free" depends on labelling rules (Module 9); the safe menu practice is to state the traces.
