# Measurement drill sheet (lessons 01.1, 01.3 and 01.7)

Weigh every pour of water on a scale with 1 g steps (1 ml of water ≈ 1 g). Tare before each pour.

Tolerance: ±5% of the target, or ±1 g for targets under 20 ml (the scale only shows whole grams).

| Target | Tolerance on the scale |
|---|---|
| 15 ml | 14–16 g |
| 22.5 ml | 21–24 g |
| 30 ml | 29–31 g |
| 45 ml | 43–47 g |
| 60 ml | 57–63 g |

Error % = (weight − target) ÷ target × 100.
