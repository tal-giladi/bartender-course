# Call-out cards and check-back drill (lesson 08.7)

Print or copy. Your partner reads a card at random; you answer in English and Hebrew within 2 seconds. Answers are at the bottom of this page; cover them while you practise.

## Call-out situations

1. You walk behind a colleague who is pouring at the speed rail.
2. You carry a rack of glasses straight out of the glasswasher.
3. A glass breaks on the edge of the ice well.
4. You carry a paring knife from the board to the sink.
5. You come round the end of the bar, where you cannot see who is coming.
6. A server calls an order; you will make it.
7. The last bottle of tequila is empty and there is no backup.
8. You need to squeeze past a colleague at the pass.
9. You walk behind a colleague carrying a hot kettle of water for tea.
10. You carry two full ice buckets behind the other bartender.
11. A colleague asks if you heard that table 4's drinks are next.
12. You drop a glass on the floor behind the bar.

## Check-back orders (partner reads from across the room, music on)

1. "Three Moscow Mules and one virgin Mule for table 7."

3. "A Negroni up, not on the rocks, and a gin and tonic with lime, table 12."
4. "Four Aperol Spritzes, one alcohol-free, terrace."
5. "Margarita, no salt, and a Paloma, table 3."
6. "Two Whiskey Sours without egg, one Old Fashioned, seat 8."
7. "Espresso Martini, decaf if we have it, and a Cosmopolitan, table 1."
