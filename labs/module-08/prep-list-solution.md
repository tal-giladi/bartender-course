The orgeat is not needed for this menu; it stays in date (10 of 28 days used) and is not counted.

Juice timing (professional judgment): press about half of the lime juice before opening (650 ml, 22 limes) and the rest mid-shift, so no juice is more than a few hours old.

Coffee beans (14 × 3 = 42) and cherries (10) need no cutting; check you have them.

## Prep order

1. Syrup (needs to cool).
2. Lemon juice, then the first half of the lime juice.
3. Cut garnish for two hours; twists last, covered on a damp cloth.
4. Mint into water; equipment, ice and glass check.

Common slips: forgetting the syrup in Daiquiri and Mojito (gives 520 ml instead of 790 ml), applying the buffer to the fridge stock, and making 900 ml of syrup when 400 ml is already in date.
