# Equipment hazard walk (lesson 08.6)

Read the description of a bar's back area at 18:40 on a Thursday, 20 minutes before opening. Find the twelve hazards. For each, write why it is dangerous and what you would do now. The bar is invented for practice.

> The barback has just put a rack of glasses into the glasswasher; the display shows a final rinse of 61 °C on a high-temperature machine. The previous rack, straight out of the machine and still steaming, has been pushed next to the ice well so the glasses are close at hand. A pint glass sits on top of the ice machine, wet, next to the bin door, which is open. Inside the bin, among the ice, three bottles of white wine are chilling for a private event. On the floor by the sink a blue bucket of sanitiser stands next to an open bottle of descaler. The soda gun's holster has a dark ring of dried syrup and something greenish at the nozzle. The draught tap for the lager pours cloudy; the line-cleaning log shows the last clean five weeks ago. A full keg sits at the top of the cellar stairs, waiting for someone to carry it down. In the cellar, two CO2 cylinders lean against the wall, unchained; one has no cap. The gas alarm panel by the cellar door shows "FAULT". A cable for the blender runs across the wet floor behind the bar, where a mat has been rolled up "to dry". The new bartender says she will "just pop down" to the cellar to change the gas, which she has never done before.
