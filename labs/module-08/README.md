# Module 8 labs — Bar Operations and Speed

Worksheets, data and run sheets for the Module 8 hands-on work and the module's practical piece (lesson 08.8, a full rush in the service simulation with a debrief and a home mini-rush). All drink practice is alcohol-free: water, tea, juices, syrups and soda stand in for spirits.

| File | Lesson | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `opening-closing-worksheet.md` | 08.1 | Your opening and closing checklist, temperature log, briefing and handover note |
| `prep-forecast.csv` | 08.2 | A Friday forecast of 97 cocktails with juice, syrup and garnish per drink |
| `prep-list-worksheet.md` | 08.2 | Turn the forecast into a prep list (buffer, fridge stock, fruit and sugar) |
| `prep-list-solution.md` | 08.2 | Worked answers |
| `batch-worksheet.md` | 08.3 | Five batching problems: pre-mix vs pre-diluted, water, serving, ABV, fridge or freezer |
| `batch-worksheet-solution.md` | 08.3 | Worked answers (match the recipe scaler's `batch` preset) |
| `inventory-week.csv` | 08.4 | One week of counts, deliveries, POS sales and a waste log for eight bottles |
| `inventory-worksheet.md` | 08.4 | Usage, variance, unexplained variance, order quantities, weigh-count |
| `inventory-worksheet-solution.md` | 08.4 | Worked answers and likely causes |
| `ticket-sequencing.md` | 08.5 | Five queue and sequencing problems using the service simulation's time table |
| `ticket-sequencing-solution.md` | 08.5 | Worked answers |
| `equipment-hazard-walk.md` | 08.6 | Find twelve hazards in a described bar; write daily-check cards |
| `equipment-hazard-walk-solution.md` | 08.6 | Hazards, reasons and actions |
| `call-out-cards.md` | 08.7 | Twelve call-out situations, eight check-back orders, two writing tasks (answers at the bottom) |
| `rush-run-sheet.md` | 08.8 | Module practical: plan, run log, debrief causes, home mini-rush tickets, reflection |
| `rush-debrief-example-solution.md` | 08.8 | One example debrief of two rush runs — compare after writing your own |

Templates used alongside: `templates/station-checklist.md`, `templates/inventory-count.md`, `templates/service-debrief.md`, `templates/practice-log.md`.

Simulations: `service-sim` presets `tutorial`, `practice` (08.5), `stockout` (08.4, 08.8), `rush` (08.8); `recipe-scaler` preset `batch` (08.3).

Data: the forecast, inventory week and debrief numbers are invented for practice, not data from a real bar. Formulas match `simulations/common/bar-math.js` (`batch`, `usage`, `variance`, `variancePercent`, `orderQuantity`). Specs are the IBA specs used in Modules 5 and 6 (checked 2026-10-17). Freezing points: Engineering ToolBox; spirit density: Canada Revenue Agency alcoholometric table; par formula: BinWise (industry source). Units: 1 oz = 30 ml (bar convention), bar spoon 5 ml, dash 0.8 ml.
