# Back bar of 30 — reference solution (lesson 02.9)

The 30 products are fictional label descriptions written in the style of real labels; they are not real brands. Score one point per row when category and style are right and the warning type is right (30 points). 24 or more (80%) is the target. Storage and substitutes are judged against the reasoning below; a different sensible substitute with the right guest message is also correct.

## Points to notice

- Only 27 and 30 carry the "other" warning; the vermouths at 16% and 18% are already "strong drinks" under the 15.5% threshold.
- 7 is a classic trap: a "spiced" product at 35% cannot be called rum in the EU and is not a swap for plain rum.
- 10 is a second trap: "reposado" without "100% agave" is in the mixto category.
- Every wine-based product (19, 28, 29, 30) goes in the fridge once opened.
