# ABV and standard-drink worksheet (lesson 02.1)

Formulas: grams of alcohol = ml × ABV × 0.789. Standard drinks = grams ÷ 14 (Israeli Ministry of Health uses about 14 g). ABV of a mix = total ml of alcohol ÷ total ml of drink. US proof = 2 × ABV.

Round grams to one decimal and standard drinks to two decimals.
