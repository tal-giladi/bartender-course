## Data notes

- The 30 back-bar products and the 20 drill items are **fictional descriptions** written in the style of real labels. They are not real brands, so no product facts are invented about real producers.
- Legal categories, minimum ABVs and age rules used in the solutions come from the sources cited in each lesson (EU Regulation 2019/787, US 27 CFR part 5, Scotch Whisky Regulations 2009, the tequila regulator, and Israel's alcohol advertising and warning-label law), checked 2026-10-17.
- The 15.5% "strong drink" threshold for the Hebrew warning is statutory (Israeli law, 2012).
- The standard drink in the ABV worksheet is about 14 g, as used by the Israeli Ministry of Health information sheet.
