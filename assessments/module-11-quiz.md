# Module 11 quiz — Final Assessment

This quiz is the written knowledge exam of the final assessment. Its ten scenario questions span the whole course: ice and glass safety, spirits and substitutions, batching and dilution, choosing a method, classic specs and their variations, the sour pattern, opening sparkling wine, sequencing a ticket, checking ID under Israeli law, and pour cost and markup. Take it closed book, in one sitting of about 30 minutes, with a calculator for the last question. The pass mark is 70%. Your score also counts for half of the written knowledge component of your final grade, together with your lesson-quiz average and your guest scenarios exam ([lesson 11.1](../lessons/module-11/lesson-01.md)); write it on your exam score sheet. If you miss a question, the explanation names the idea to revisit.
