# Module 10 quiz — Costing and Menu Design

This quiz checks whether you can make the money decisions behind a bar menu in realistic situations: taking VAT out of a purchase price, costing a drink with waste allowances and juice yield, counting servings per bottle, telling pour cost, margin and markup apart, setting a VAT-inclusive menu price from a target, knowing what Israeli law requires of a menu price and description, turning POS sales into theoretical usage and reconciling stock in shekels, reading a menu-engineering table, upselling responsibly, and judging a whole menu by its sales mix. It has 10 scenario questions and most need a short calculation, so keep a calculator ready. The pass mark is 70%. If you miss a question, the explanation tells you which idea to revisit.
