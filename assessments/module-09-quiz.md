# Module 9 quiz — Customer Service, Responsible Service and Israeli Requirements

This quiz checks whether you can make the right call with real guests: acknowledging and checking back orders, handling a complaint and an unclear order, answering an allergy question honestly with a clean set, spotting intoxication and refusing service safely, checking ID against the law's presumption, knowing what the 23:00 sale ban and a bar's licence conditions mean behind the bar, applying the smoking law, and keeping food and workplace safety rules. Several questions ask which rule applies and whether it is statutory, a licence condition or house policy. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70%. If you miss a question, the explanation tells you which idea to revisit. Israeli legal references were checked on 2026-10-18.
