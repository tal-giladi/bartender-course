# Module 4 quiz — Mixing Methods and Technical Skills

This quiz checks whether you can choose and use the right technique in real bar situations: picking build, stir, shake or blend from an ingredient list, keeping a highball fizzy, stirring and shaking to a consistent dilution, making a safe egg-white or aquafaba foam, straining, muddling and layering, blending and throwing safely, garnishing hygienically, and judging your own technique from measured evidence. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70%. If you miss a question, the explanation tells you which idea to revisit.
