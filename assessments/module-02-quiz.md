# Module 2 quiz — Alcohol and Beverage Knowledge

This quiz checks whether you can use what Module 2 taught in real bar situations: working out how strong a serve is, reading labels, choosing the right product and a fair substitute, storing opened bottles, and answering guests' questions about spirits, liqueurs, wine-based products, beer and alcohol-free drinks. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70%. If you miss a question, the explanation tells you which idea to revisit.
