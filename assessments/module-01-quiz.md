# Module 1 quiz — Bar Foundations and Equipment

This quiz checks how you apply Module 1 at a real station: choosing tools, measuring, handling knives, glass and ice, and keeping the station clean through setup, service and close. The questions are short scenarios; pick what a careful working bartender would do. Pass mark: 70%. You can retake it.
