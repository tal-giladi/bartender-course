# Module 6 quiz — Classic Cocktails II and Menu Knowledge

This quiz checks whether you can use cocktail families and the Module 6 specs in real bar situations: sorting an unfamiliar drink into its family, pouring the IBA White Lady, Sidecar, Pisco Sour, French 75, Tom Collins, Paloma, Bloody Mary, Mai Tai and the more recent classics correctly, keeping a sparkling top lively, handling egg and allergen requests safely, substituting and adapting honestly, clearing up drink names that mean different things in different places, separating verified facts about Israeli menus from professional judgment, and building a balanced menu. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70%. If you miss a question, the explanation tells you which idea to revisit.
