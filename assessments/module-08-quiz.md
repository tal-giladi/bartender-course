# Module 8 quiz — Bar Operations and Speed

This quiz checks whether you can run the operational side of a bar in realistic situations: opening and closing in the right order, acting on a fridge temperature, turning a forecast into a prep list, deciding what goes into a batch and how much water it needs, keeping a batch out of a freezer that would freeze it, reconciling stock and ordering up to par, choosing which ticket to make next and sequencing a round, using commercial equipment safely (including what to do when a cellar gas alarm sounds), and communicating with call-outs, check-backs and 86s. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70%. If you miss a question, the explanation tells you which idea to revisit.
