# Module 7 quiz — Beer, Wine, Sparkling Drinks and Beverage Service

This quiz checks whether you can serve beer, wine, sparkling and alcohol-free drinks well in real bar situations: choosing the temperature and glass for a beer, pouring a draught beer and telling your mistakes from a system fault, describing wines in guest language, reading Israeli wine labels including kosher and mevushal marks and the 2026 region map, opening still and sparkling wine safely, reading alcohol-free labels exactly, and recommending a drink when the request is really a safety question. It has 10 scenario questions. The pass mark is 70%. If you miss a question, the explanation tells you which idea to revisit.
