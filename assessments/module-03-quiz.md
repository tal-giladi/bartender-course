# Module 3 quiz — Measurement, Dilution and Flavor Science

This quiz checks how you use Module 3 at a real station: converting and scaling recipes, measuring accurately, balancing sweet, sour, bitter and salt, working with syrups and juice, controlling dilution and temperature, keeping drinks fizzy, and diagnosing a drink that tastes wrong. The questions are short scenarios with some light calculation; pick what a careful working bartender would do. Pass mark: 70%. You can retake it.
